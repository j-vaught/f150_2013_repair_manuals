501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Disassembly and Assembly Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Backrest — Rear, SuperCab

Disassembly

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Rear, SuperCab in this section.

  1. Remove the seat backrest cover. For additional information, refer to Seat Backrest Cover — Rear, SuperCab in this section.
  1. Push the release tabs and remove the 3 backrest plastic lock clips (one shown).
  1. To assemble, reverse the disassembly procedure.
