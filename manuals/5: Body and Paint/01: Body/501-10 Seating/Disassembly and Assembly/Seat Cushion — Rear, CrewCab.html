501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Disassembly and Assembly Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Cushion — Rear, CrewCab

Disassembly

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Rear, Crew Cab.

  1. Remove the seat cushion latch. Refer to Seat Latch — Rear, Crew Cab.
  1. Remove the cushion foam pad.
    • If equipped, disconnect the cushion heater mat electrical connector.
      • Note the wire harness routing for correct installation.
  1. Remove the bolt and safety belt buckle.
    • To install, tighten to 32 Nm (24 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the screw and inboard side shield.
  1. Remove the inboard pivot bolt, inboard pedestal and cushion.
    • To install, tighten to 32 Nm (24 lb-ft).
  1. To assemble, reverse the disassembly procedure.
