501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Dual Climate Controlled Seat Module (DCSM)

Removal and Installation

  1. When installing a new Dual Climate Controlled Seat Module (DCSM) , carry out the appropriate steps in the Programmable Module Installation (PMI) procedure. For additional information, refer to Section  418-01.
  1. Remove the front passenger seat. For additional information, refer to Seat — Front in this section.
  1. Disconnect the 3 DCSM electrical connectors.
    • Release the 2 locking wedges.
    • Disconnect the 3 connectors.
  1. Remove the 2 screws and DCSM .
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. If a new DCSM has been installed, carry out the appropriate steps to complete the Programmable Module Installation (PMI) procedure. For additional information, refer to Section  418-01.
