501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Backrest Thermo-Electric Device

Removal and Installation

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Front in this section.

  1. Position the seat backrest fully forward.
  1. Release the backrest cover J-clip and unzip the 2 zippers.
  1. Disconnect the Thermo-Electric Device (TED) electrical connector.
  1. Detach the pin-type retainer attaching the TED duct to the backrest frame.
  1. Un-hook the TED from the backrest support wire and remove.
    • If equipped with power lumbar, note cable routing for correct installation.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
