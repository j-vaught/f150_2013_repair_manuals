501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Cushion Cover — Rear, SuperCab

Removal and Installation

NOTE: 40 percent and 60 percent rear seat cushion covers are removed in the same manor.

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Rear, SuperCab in this section.

  1. Remove the rear seat cushion. For additional information, refer to Seat — Rear, SuperCab in this section.
  1. Release the cushion cover J-clip.

  1. NOTICE: Use care when separating the seat cushion cover from the hook-and-loop strip, or the hook-and-loop strip can be torn from the cushion foam pad.

    Invert the cushion cover to the row of hog rings.
  1. Remove the hog rings.
  1. Invert and remove the cushion cover.
    • If needed, separate and remove foam pad from the cushion frame.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
