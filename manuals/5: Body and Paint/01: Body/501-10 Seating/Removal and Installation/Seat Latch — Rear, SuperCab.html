501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Latch — Rear, SuperCab

Removal and Installation

NOTE: 40 percent and 60 percent rear seat cushion latches are removed in the same manor.

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Rear, SuperCab in this section.

  1. Remove the rear seat. For additional information, refer to Seat — Rear, SuperCab in this section.
  1. Remove the cushion pivot bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 33 Nm (24 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the rear screw from the outboard riser.
    • Separate the cushion from the inboard riser to avoid pivot bushing damage.
    • To install, tighten to 6 Nm (53 lb-in).
  1. Remove the rear latch bolt and outboard seat riser.
    • To install, tighten to 24 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the screw and latch bolt cover.
  1. Remove the front latch bolt and cushion latch.
    • To install, tighten to 32 Nm (24 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
