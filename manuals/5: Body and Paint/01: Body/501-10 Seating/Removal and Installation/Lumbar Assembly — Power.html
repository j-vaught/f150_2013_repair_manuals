501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Lumbar Assembly — Power

Removal and Installation

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Front in this section.

  1. Release the backrest cover J-clip and unzip the 2 zippers.
  1. If equipped, remove the backrest Thermo-Electric Device (TED) . For additional information, refer to Seat Backrest Thermo-Electric Device in this section.
  1. Disconnect the lumbar motor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the lumbar assembly.
    • Pinch the lumbar retainers together to release from the backrest frame.
    • For installation, make sure the arrow is pointed up.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
