501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Backrest Cover — Front, Center

Removal and Installation

NOTE: The seat backrest cover on a backrest equipped with storage tray is not serviceable separately. A new backrest must be installed. For additional information, refer to Seat Backrest — Front, Center in this section.

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Front, Center in this section.

  1. Remove the seat backrest. For additional information, refer to Seat Backrest — Front, Center in this section.
  1. Un-tuck the zipper and open the backrest cover.
  1. Invert the backrest cover and remove the hog rings.
  1. Remove the backrest cover.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
