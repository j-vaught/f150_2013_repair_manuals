501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Backrest — Front

Removal and Installation

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Front in this section.

All seats

  1. Remove the seat. For additional information, refer to Seat — Front in this section.
  1. For SuperCab, remove the manual recline handle, if equipped.
    • Insert an appropriate tool behind the cushion side shield, release the retainer and remove the handle.
  1. If equipped, remove the adjustable lumbar knob.
    • Adjust the lumbar until fully relaxed.
    • Release the retainer and remove the knob.
  1. Remove the side shield insert.
    • Release the lower retainers and lift to release the upper retainers.
    • If equipped, disconnect the seat control switch and memory SET switch electrical connectors.
  1. Remove the side shield.
    • Remove the front and rear side shield screws.
    • Pull the side shield outward to release the retainers and remove.
    • Reinstall all retainers back to the side shield.
  1. Release the cushion cover rear J-clip and rear flaps.
  1. If equipped, detach the cable from the lumbar adjuster, release the pin-type retainer and route out the cable from the cushion frame.
    • Note cable routing for correct installation.

Power and climate controlled seats

  1. Disconnect the backrest wiring harness electrical connector and detach the wiring harness pin-type retainers from the seat track.
    • Note wiring harness routing for correct installation.

All seats

  1. Disconnect and detach the side air bag wiring harness electrical connector from the seat track.
    • Note wiring harness routing for correct installation.

  1. WARNING: Use care when the seat back frame is removed and when releasing the recliners from a backrest upright position. The recliners are spring-loaded, which may cause the recliner upper arms to fold with significant speed/force. Failure to follow this instruction may result in serious personal injury.

    Remove the 4 bolts and seat backrest.
    • To install, tighten to 45 Nm (33 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
