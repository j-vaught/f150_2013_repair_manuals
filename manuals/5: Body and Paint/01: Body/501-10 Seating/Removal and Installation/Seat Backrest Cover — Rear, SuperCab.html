501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Backrest Cover — Rear, SuperCab

Removal and Installation

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Rear, SuperCab in this section.

  1. Remove the rear seat backrest. For additional information, refer to Seat — Rear, SuperCab in this section.
  1. Push the release buttons and remove the 3 head restraints.
  1. Remove and discard the 6 head restraint guide sleeves. For additional information, refer to Head Restraint Guide Sleeve in this section.
    • To install, install 6 new head restraint guide sleeves.
  1. Release all the backrest cover J-clips and retainer strips.

  1. NOTICE: Use care when separating the backrest cover from the hook-and-loop strip, or the hook-and-loop strip can be torn from the foam pad.

    Invert the backrest cover and separate the hook-and-loop strips.
  1. Remove the hog rings and backrest cover.
    • If required, remove the hog rings from the backrest frame and separate from the foam pad.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
