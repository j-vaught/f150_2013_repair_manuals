501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Latch — Rear, Crew Cab

Removal and Installation

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Rear, Crew Cab in this section.

  1. Remove the seat cushion cover. For additional information, refer to Seat Cushion Cover — Rear, Crew Cab in this section.
  1. Remove the 2 screws and latch release handle.
    • Release the cable retainer.
    • Note cable routing for correct installation.
  1. For 60 percent seat, detach the assist spring strut from the outboard riser.
  1. Remove the front and rear latch bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 32 Nm (24 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the cushion pivot bolt, outboard riser and cushion latch.
    • To install, tighten to 32 Nm (24 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
