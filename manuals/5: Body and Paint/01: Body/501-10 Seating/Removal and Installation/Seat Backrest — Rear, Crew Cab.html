501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Backrest — Rear, Crew Cab

Removal and Installation

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Rear, Crew Cab in this section.

  1. Remove the seat. For additional information, refer to Seat — Rear, Crew Cab, 40 Percent or Seat — Rear, Crew Cab, 60 Percent in this section.
  1. Remove the 2 screws and inboard side shield.
  1. Remove the screw and outboard side shield.
    • If detached, return the retainer clip to the side shield before installation.
  1. If equipped, disconnect the cushion heater mat electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 2 backrest pivot bolts and backrest.
    • To install, tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the installation procedure.
