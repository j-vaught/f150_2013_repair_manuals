501-10 Seating 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Latch — Front, Center

Removal and Installation

NOTE: For parts identification and location, refer to Seat — Exploded View, Front, Center in this section.

  1. Remove the seat. For additional information, refer to Seat — Front, Center in this section.
  1. Remove the rear cupholder.
    • Remove the 2 rear cupholder screws.
    • If equipped, disconnect the power point electrical connector.
  1. Remove the backrest latch cover.
  1. Remove the RH side shield.
    • Release the pin-type retainer.
  1. Remove the 2 backrest latch upper bolts.
    • Lower the backrest to avoid damaging the pivot side.
    • To install, tighten to 60 Nm (44 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the 2 backrest latch lower bolts and backrest latch.
    • To install, tighten to 45 Nm (33 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
