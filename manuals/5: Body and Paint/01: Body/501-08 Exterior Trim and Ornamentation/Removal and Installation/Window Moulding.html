501-08 Exterior Trim and Ornamentation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Window Moulding

NOTE: LH side shown, RH side similar.


Item Part Number Description
1 2259622596 RH/ 22597 LH RH/ 22597 LH Outer belt moulding
2 2055420554 RH/ 20555 LH RH/ 20555 LH Window moulding
3 14521452 RH/ 1453 LH RH/ 1453 LH Glass run

Removal

  1. Remove the outer belt moulding.
    • Release the retaining clip at the rear of the belt moulding, then slide the front of the moulding off of the flange to remove the belt moulding from the door.

  1. NOTE: The window moulding is held on by double-sided tape from the forward edge of the moulding and by trim clips near the center of the moulding. The rearmost edge rolls over the door frame sheet metal.

    Starting at the upper forward corner of the moulding, insert a suitable removal tool between the moulding and the door frame.
  1. Determine the location of the double-sided tape. Carefully spread spray-on adhesive cleaner down the space between the applique and door frame. Allow the cleaner to set for several minutes.
  1. Using the removal tool, begin removing the moulding, spraying additional adhesive cleaner as needed.

Installation

  1. Remove all remaining adhesive tape on the mating surfaces of the door and apply new adhesive tape.

  1. NOTE: Make sure the forward edge of the moulding is underneath the glass run lip.

    Install the window moulding on the edge of the door, engage the groove then gently press down to engage the clips and tape.
  1. Install the outer belt moulding.
