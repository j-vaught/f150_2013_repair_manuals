501-08 Exterior Trim and Ornamentation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 11/08/2012

Fender Moulding — Front

Removal and Installation

Standard

  1. Remove the 2 front fender moulding nuts.
    • Tighten to 3.5 Nm (31 Ib-in).
  1. Remove the 5 front fender moulding pin-type retainers.
    • Starting at the front of the fender moulding, pull outward to release moulding from the fender.
  1. Using a non-marring trim tool, carefully pry the moulding outward to gain access to the forward pin-type retainers.

  1. NOTICE: Pry directly on the pin-type retainer. Do not pry on the fender moulding. Failure to follow this direction may result in damage to the component.

    Using a non-marring tool, release the front fender moulding front pin-type retainers.
    • Pry outward on the first front fender moulding front pin-type retainer by pushing the handle toward the fender.
    • Pry outward on the second front fender moulding front pin-type retainer by pushing the handle toward the fender.
    • Follow this sequence for the remaining retainers.
  1. Remove the front fender moulding.
    • Pull outward to release the rear fender moulding pin-type retainers.

SVT Raptor

  1. Remove the 5 front fender moulding pin-type retainers.
  1. Using a non-marring trim tool, carefully pry the moulding outward to gain access to the forward pin-type retainers.

  1. NOTICE: Pry directly on the pin-type retainer. Do not pry on the fender moulding. Failure to follow this direction may result in damage to the component.

    Using a non-marring tool, release the front fender moulding front pin-type retainers.
    • Pry outward on the first front fender moulding front pin-type retainer by pushing the handle toward the fender.
    • Pry outward on the second front fender moulding front pin-type retainer by pushing the handle toward the fender.
    • Follow this sequence for the remaining retainers.
  1. Remove the front fender moulding.
    • Pull outward to release the rear fender moulding pin-type retainers.

All vehicles

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
