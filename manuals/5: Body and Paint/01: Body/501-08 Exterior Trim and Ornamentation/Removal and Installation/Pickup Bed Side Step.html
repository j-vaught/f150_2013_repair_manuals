Removal and Installation

Side step assembly

NOTE: The pickup bed side step must be closed to complete this procedure.

  1. Remove the pickup bed side step bracket slide strap support bracket-to-side step bracket bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 55 Nm (41 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the 4 pickup bed side step-to-frame bolts.
    • Tighten to 55 Nm (41 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the locating nut.
    • To install, tighten to 55 Nm (41 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the pickup bed side step assembly.

Side step body

  1. Remove the pickup bed side step body upper retainer bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 55 Nm (41 lb-ft).
  1. Loosen the pickup bed side step body lower nuts.
    • To install, tighten to 55 Nm (41 lb-ft).

  1. NOTE: Pull outward to release the step body from the pickup bed side step bracket studs.

    Remove the pickup bed side step body.

All procedures

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
