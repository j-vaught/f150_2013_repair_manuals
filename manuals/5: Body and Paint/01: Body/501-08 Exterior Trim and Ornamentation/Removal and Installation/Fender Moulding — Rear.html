501-08 Exterior Trim and Ornamentation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Fender Moulding — Rear

Removal and Installation

Standard

  1. Remove the 2 rear fender moulding screws.
  1. Remove the 4 rear fender moulding pin-type retainers.
  1. Starting at the front of the fender moulding, pull outward to release it from the fender.

SVT Raptor

  1. Remove the 6 rear fender moulding pin-type retainers.
  1. Remove the rear fender moulding screws.

  1. NOTICE: Pry directly on the pin-type retainer. Do not pry on the fender moulding. Failure to follow this direction may result in damage to the component.

    Using a non-marring tool, starting at the front of the rear fender moulding, pry outward on the pin-type retainers by pushing the handle toward the fender.
  1. Remove the rear fender moulding.

All vehicles

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
