501-35 Body Repairs 2013 F-150
Description and Operation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Body

WARNING: Collision damage repair must conform to the instructions contained in this workshop manual. Replacement components must be new, genuine Ford Motor Company parts. Recycled, salvaged, aftermarket or reconditioned parts (including body parts, wheels or safety restraint components) are not authorized by Ford.

Departure from the instructions provided in this manual, including alternate repair methods or the use of substitute components, risks compromising crash safety. Failure to follow these instructions may adversely affect structural integrity and crash safety performance, which could result in serious personal injury to vehicle occupants in a crash.

The body consists of the following:

  • Cab-forward design
  • Full frame bolted to the body
  • Aluminum hood
  • Body side outer panel constructed of dent resistant High-Strength Steel (HSS) and mild steels
  • Magnesium radiator support
  • Front structure inner reinforcement is hydroformed High-Strength Low Alloy (HSLA) rail assembly
  • Lightweight steel welded panel construction
  • Extensive use of HSLA , Dual Phase Steel (DP) and Boron steel in the cab body structure(s)
  • Dash panel constructed of laminated sound/vibration steel
  • Bolted, removable front fenders, hinged doors, tailgate and hood
  • Fender outer panels constructed of dent resistant HSS steel
  • Front and rear door outer panels constructed of dent resistant HSS steel
  • Structural adhesive used throughout the body shell and underbody
  • Regular cab, SuperCab and SuperCrew cab styles
  • Styleside pickup bed available with 1.67 m (5.5 ft), 1.98 m (6.5 ft) and 2.44 m (8.0 ft) pickup bed length
  • 3,198 mm (126 in) wheelbase
  • 3,365 mm (133 in) wheelbase
  • 3,671 mm (145 in) wheelbase
  • 3,988 mm (157 in) wheelbase
  • 4,140 mm (163 in) wheelbase

  • NOTE: For information regarding cab style and pickup bed configurations, refer to Section  100-01.

    Platinum edition available in SuperCrew cab style only
  • Platinum edition has unique chrome body side cladding
  • Platinum edition has unique chrome pickup bed rail bars
  • Mastic sound deadener material used on floor pans

F-150 SVT Raptor package contains the following:

  • SuperCab
  • 1.67 m (5.5 ft) pickup bed length
  • Steel pickup bed outer panels
  • 3,365 mm (133 in) wheelbase
  • Sheet-Molded Composite (SMC) hood and front fenders
  • Unique grille
  • Modified front and rear bumpers

Lincoln Mark LT (Mexico only) contains the following:

  • Lincoln Mark LT available in SuperCrew cab, styleside
  • Lincoln Mark LT has unique chrome grille
  • Lincoln Mark LT has unique chrome body side cladding
  • Lincoln Mark LT has unique chrome pickup bed rail bars
  • Lincoln Mark LT has unique tailgate applique
