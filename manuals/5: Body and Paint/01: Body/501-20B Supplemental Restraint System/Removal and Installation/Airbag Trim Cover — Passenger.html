501-20B Supplemental Restraint System 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 01/27/2015

Airbag Trim Cover — Passenger


General Equipment
Flat bladed screwdriver

Removal

WARNING: Always carry a live airbag with the deployment door, trim cover or tear seam pointed away from the body. Do not place a live airbag down with the deployment door, trim cover or tear seam facing down. Failure to follow these instructions may result in serious personal injury in the event of an accidental deployment.

WARNING: Do not unpack or unroll the passenger airbag soft pack. If the soft pack becomes unpacked or unrolled, install a new passenger airbag assembly. Failure to follow these instructions may result in the passenger airbag deploying incorrectly and increases the risk of serious personal injury or death in a crash.

WARNING: Never probe the electrical connectors on airbag, Safety Canopy or side air curtain assemblies. Failure to follow this instruction may result in the accidental deployment of these assemblies, which increases the risk of serious personal injury or death.

  1. Remove the passenger airbag. Passenger Airbag.

  1. NOTE: Carry out these actions until all 4 passenger airbag retainers are positioned through to the other side of the passenger airbag cover.

    Detach the passenger airbag retainers from the passenger airbag cover.
    1. Position the screwdriver between the passenger airbag retainer and the passenger airbag cover opening.
    1. Push down on the screwdriver handle making room for the passenger airbag retainer to pass through the passenger airbag cover opening.
    1. Position the passenger airbag retainer through to the other side of the passenger airbag cover opening.
  1. Rotate the passenger airbag out of the passenger airbag cover and remove it.

Installation

  1. WARNING: Carefully inspect the passenger airbag trim cover, canister and soft pack before assembly. If any foreign objects are found, remove them before attaching the passenger airbag trim cover to the canister. If the canister or soft pack is damaged, install a new passenger airbag assembly. Failure to follow this instruction may result in the passenger airbag deploying incorrectly, which increases the risk of serious personal injury or death in a crash.

    1. Position the passenger airbag to the passenger airbag cover.
    1. Engage the 5 passenger airbag retainers to the passenger airbag cover.
  1. Rotate the passenger airbag down and into the passenger airbag cover.
  1. Engage the 4 passenger airbag retainers to the passenger airbag cover.
  1. Install the passenger airbag. Passenger Airbag.
