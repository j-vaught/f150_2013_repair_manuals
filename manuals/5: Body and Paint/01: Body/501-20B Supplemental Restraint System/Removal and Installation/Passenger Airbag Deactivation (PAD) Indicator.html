501-20B Supplemental Restraint System 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Passenger Airbag Deactivation (PAD) Indicator


Item Part Number Description
1 0430204302 Instrument panel center finish panel
2 1335013350 Passenger Air Bag Deactivation (PAD) indicator

NOTE: The Passenger Air Bag Deactivation (PAD) indicator is part of the hazard/ traction control switch.

  1. NOTE: Ignition must be OFF when disconnecting the PAD indicator electrical connector. Otherwise, a DTC will be set in the Restraints Control Module (RCM) memory and must be cleared before releasing the vehicle.

    Turn the ignition OFF and wait at least one minute.
  1. Remove the instrument panel center finish panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Disconnect the PAD indicator electrical connector.
  1. Press the 2 tabs inward and remove the PAD indicator.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
