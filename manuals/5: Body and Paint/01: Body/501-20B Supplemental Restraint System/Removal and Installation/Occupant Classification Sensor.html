501-20B Supplemental Restraint System 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Occupant Classification Sensor

Removal

  1. NOTE: The Occupant Classification System (OCS) is part of the passenger seat track assembly and is not serviced separately.

    Remove the passenger seat track. For additional information, refer to the Seat Track removal and installation procedures in Section  501-10.
