501-20B Supplemental Restraint System 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Seat Position Sensor


Item Part Number Description
1 — Lock tab
2 14B41614B416 Seat position sensor
3 — Electrical connector (part of 14A699)

  1. Remove the driver seat. For additional information, refer to Seat Removal and Installation in Section  501-10.
  1. Disconnect the seat position sensor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the lock tab.
  1. Remove the seat position sensor.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
