501-20B Supplemental Restraint System 2013 F-150
General Procedures Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Supplemental Restraint System (SRS) Deactivation and Reactivation


Special Tool(s)
Vehicle Communication Module (VCM) and Integrated Diagnostic System (IDS)
software with appropriate hardware, or equivalent scan tool

Deactivation

WARNING: Never probe the electrical connectors on airbag, Safety Canopy or side air curtain assemblies. Failure to follow this instruction may result in the accidental deployment of these assemblies, which increases the risk of serious personal injury or death.

WARNING: Never disassemble or tamper with seatbelt deployable components, including pretensioners, load limiters and inflators. Never back probe deployable device electrical connectors. Tampering or back probing may cause an accidental deployment and result in personal injury or death.

WARNING: Do not handle, move or change the original horizontal mounting position of the restraints control module (RCM) while the RCM is connected and the ignition switch is ON. Failure to follow this instruction may result in the accidental deployment of the Safety Canopy and cause serious personal injury or death.

NOTE: The Supplemental Restraint System (SRS) must be fully operational and free of faults before releasing the vehicle to the customer.

  1. Depower the SRS . For additional information, refer to Supplemental Restraint System (SRS) Depowering and Repowering in this section.

  1. WARNING: Always deplete the backup power supply before repairing or installing any new front or side air bag supplemental restraint system (SRS) component and before servicing, removing, installing, adjusting or striking components near the front or side impact sensors or the restraints control module (RCM). Nearby components include doors, instrument panel, console, door latches, strikers, seats and hood latches. Refer to the Description and Operation portion of Section  501-20B for location of the RCM and impact sensor(s). To deplete the backup power supply energy, disconnect the battery ground cable and wait at least 1 minute. Be sure to disconnect auxiliary batteries and power supplies (if equipped). Failure to follow these instructions may result in serious personal injury or death in the event of an accidental deployment.

    Disconnect the battery ground cable and wait at least one minute. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Remove the driver air bag module. For additional information, refer to Driver Airbag in this section.
  1. Remove the 3 passenger air bag module bolts.

  1. NOTICE: Do not handle the passenger air bag module by grabbing the edges of the passenger air bag cover. Damage to the passenger air bag module may occur.

    Partially remove the passenger air bag module from the instrument panel.
  1. Disconnect the 2 passenger air bag module electrical connectors.
  1. Under the passenger seat, slide and disengage the locking clip, release the tab and disconnect inline electrical connector C339.
  1. Under the driver seat, slide and disengage the locking clip, release the tab and disconnect inline electrical connector C3051.
  1. Disconnect the LH side safety canopy module electrical connector.
    • For regular cab, remove the LH side B-pillar trim panel.
    • For SuperCab and SuperCrew, remove the LH side C-pillar trim panel.
      For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Disconnect the RH side safety canopy module electrical connector.
    • For regular cab, remove the RH side B-pillar trim panel.
    • For SuperCab and SuperCrew, remove the RH side C-pillar trim panel.
      For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Install the RCM fuse 31 (10A) in the SJB .
  1. Connect the battery ground cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.

Reactivation

  1. Remove RCM fuse 31 (10A) from the SJB .
  1. Disconnect the battery ground cable and wait at least one minute.
  1. Connect the LH side safety canopy module electrical connector.
    • For regular cab, install the LH side B-pillar trim panel.
    • For SuperCab and SuperCrew, install the LH side C-pillar trim panel.
      For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Connect the RH side safety canopy module electrical connector.
    • For regular cab, install the RH side B-pillar trim panel.
    • For SuperCab and SuperCrew, install the RH side C-pillar trim panel.
      For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Under the driver seat, connect inline electrical connector C339, slide and engage the locking clip.
  1. Under the driver seat, connect inline electrical connector C3051, slide and engage the locking clip.
  1. Connect the 2 passenger air bag module electrical connectors.
  1. Position the passenger air bag module so that the tabs are oriented as shown.
  1. Install the 3 passenger air bag module bolts.
    • Tighten to 8 Nm (71 lb-in).
  1. Install the driver air bag module. Refer to Driver Airbag in this section.
  1. Repower the SRS . For additional information, refer to Supplemental Restraint System (SRS) Depowering and Repowering in this section.
