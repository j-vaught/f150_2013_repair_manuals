Removal and Installation

All vehicles

  1. Remove the headlamps. For additional information, refer to Section  417-01.
  1. Remove the 6 front bumper cover-to-front fender bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 3 Nm (27 lb-in).

  1. NOTE: Any time the bumper cover-to-bumper bracket bolts are loosened or removed, new bolts must be installed.

    Remove and discard the 2 bumper cover-to-bumper bracket bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 3 Nm (27 lb-in).

Except F-150 SVT Raptor

  1. Remove the 10 front bumper cover-to-bumper cover bracket pin-type retainers.

F-150 SVT Raptor

  1. Remove the 8 front bumper cover-to-bumper cover bracket pin-type retainers.

Vehicles equipped with fender mouldings

  1. Remove the fender mouldings. For additional information, refer to Section  501-08.

All vehicles

  1. NOTE: The rearward portion of the front bumper cover must be pulled upward and outward to release it from the bumper cover brackets.

    Remove the front bumper cover.
  1. Inspect the bumper cover brackets for damage. If necessary, install new bumper cover brackets.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Install the 2 new bumper cover-to-bumper bolts and tighten to 3 Nm (27 lb-in).
