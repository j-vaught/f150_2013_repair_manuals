Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the hood release retaining bolt.
  1. Position aside the hood release assembly.
    • Disconnect the hood release cable routing retainer.
  1. Remove the 2 bumper cover-to-bumper bracket bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 3 Nm (27 lb-in).
  1. If equipped, disconnect the fog lamp electrical connectors.

  1. NOTE: To assist with bumper alignment, mark the 4 bumper mounting nuts to the bumper bracket.

    Remove the 4 front bumper nuts.
    • To install, tighten to 115 Nm (85 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the front bumper.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
