501-09 Rear View Mirrors 2013 F-150
Description and Operation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Rear View Mirrors

Exterior Mirrors

Aero Exterior Mirrors

The main serviceable components of the aero exterior mirrors are:

  • Exterior mirror glass
  • Exterior mirror plastic housing
  • Exterior mirror control switch (optional)
  • Heated exterior mirror glass (optional)
  • Exterior mirror glass (blind spot optional, LH only)
  • LH auto-dimming exterior mirror glass (optional)
  • Exterior mirror glass adjustment motors (optional)
  • Exterior mirror cover (plastic, chrome or painted)
  • Exterior mirror glass adjustment motors (optional — with memory)
  • Exterior mirror glass adjustment motor (manual) (standard)
  • Exterior mirror puddle lamps (optional)
  • Exterior mirror turn signals (optional)

Trailer Tow Exterior Mirrors

The main serviceable components of the trailer tow exterior mirrors are:

  • Exterior mirror glass
  • Exterior mirror spotter glass
  • Heated exterior mirror glass (optional)
  • Exterior mirror cover (optional — chrome or painted)
  • Exterior mirror control switch (optional)
  • Exterior telescoping mirror control switch (optional — part of the exterior mirror control switch)
  • Exterior mirror glass adjustment motors (optional — power mirrors only)
  • Exterior mirror puddle lamps (optional)
  • Exterior mirror turn signals (optional)
  • Exterior mirror side marker (optional — telescoping mirrors only)
  • Exterior mirror fold relay (power folding with telescoping mirrors only)
  • DSM (optional — memory exterior mirrors)

Interior Mirror

Interior Rear View Mirrors

The vehicle may be equipped with one of the following interior rear view mirrors:

  • Interior rear view mirror
  • Interior auto-dimming rear view mirror with compass
  • Interior auto-dimming rear view mirror with video display
  • Interior auto-dimming rear view mirror with compass and video display
