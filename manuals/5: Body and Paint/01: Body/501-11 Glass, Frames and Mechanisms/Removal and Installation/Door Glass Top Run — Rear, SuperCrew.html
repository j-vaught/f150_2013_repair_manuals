501-11 Glass, Frames and Mechanisms 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Door Glass Top Run — Rear, SuperCrew

Removal and Installation

  1. Position the rear door window glass in the full downward position.
  1. Remove the rear door trim panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Remove the rear door interior moulding.
  1. Remove the rear door window glass top run.
    • Remove the rear door window glass top run through the top opening of the rear door.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
