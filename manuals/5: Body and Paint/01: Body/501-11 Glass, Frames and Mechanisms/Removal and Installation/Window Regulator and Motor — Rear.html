501-11 Glass, Frames and Mechanisms 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Window Regulator and Motor — Rear

Removal

  1. Remove the rear window glass. For additional information, refer to Window Glass — Rear in this section.
  1. Disconnect the rear window glass motor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the rear window glass regulator and motor assembly screws.
    • To install, tighten to 11 Nm (97 lb-in).
  1. Remove the rear window glass regulator and motor assembly from the vehicle.

  1. NOTICE: If installing a new regulator and motor assembly, make sure any clips, retainers or wire ties that may have been used for shipping purposes are removed once the new regulator and motor are installed. Failure to follow this instruction may result in damage to the new regulator and motor assembly.

    To install, reverse the removal procedure.
