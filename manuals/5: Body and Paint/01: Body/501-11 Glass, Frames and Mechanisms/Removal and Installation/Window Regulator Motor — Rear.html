501-11 Glass, Frames and Mechanisms 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Window Regulator Motor — Rear

Removal

  1. For SuperCab and SuperCrew, remove the rear corner trim panels.
    • For SuperCab, remove the B-pillar trim panels. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
    • For SuperCrew, remove the C-pillar trim panels. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Remove the rear trim panel pin-type retainers and the rear trim panel.
  1. Disconnect the rear window glass motor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 3 rear window glass regulator and motor assembly screws.
  1. Carefully remove the 3 bolts attaching the steel bracket to the plastic cable housing.
  1. Hold the plastic cable housing and motor together, place a screwdriver between the drum (located in the cable housing) and the motor housing and pry the motor away from the drum, making sure the drum stays inside the plastic cable housing.

Installation

  1. Align the motor gear splines onto the drum and press the motor into place, making sure there is correct alignment of the attachment bolt holes.
    • Install the 3 bolts attaching the steel bracket to the plastic cable housing.
  1. Install the rear window glass regulator and motor assembly screws.
    • Tighten to 11 Nm (97 lb-in).
  1. Connect the rear window glass motor electrical connector.
  1. Install the rear trim panel and the pin-type retainers.
  1. For SuperCab and SuperCrew, install the rear corner trim panels.
    • For SuperCab, install the B-pillar trim panels. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
    • For SuperCrew, install the C-pillar trim panels. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
