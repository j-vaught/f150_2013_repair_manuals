501-11 Glass, Frames and Mechanisms 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Window Glass — Rear, Power Sliding Panel

NOTE: If equipped with rear window defrost, the sliding glass panel cannot be serviced separately. The rear window must be replaced as a complete assembly. For additional information, refer to Window Glass — Rear in this section.

  1. Remove the rear window glass assembly from the vehicle and place on a stable work surface. For additional information, refer to Window Glass — Rear in this section.
  1. Position the sliding glass panel to the full CLOSED position.
  1. Remove the upper rail filler.
    • Pull down the driver side of the upper filler while carefully sliding it out of the track towards the driver side of the rear glass assembly.
  1. Push the sliding glass upward and tilt the bottom inward to disengage the sliding glass from the center bracket guide. Remove the sliding glass panel.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
