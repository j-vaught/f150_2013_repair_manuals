Removal and Installation

WARNING: All seatbelt components must be inspected and corrected as part of any collision repair. Inspect all seatbelt components as prescribed by Seatbelt Procedure After a Collision found in Section 501-20A General Procedures of this manual. Failure to follow this instruction may result in incorrect operation of the seatbelt system and increase the risk of serious personal injury or death in a crash.

  1. Remove the B-pillar trim panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Remove safety belt shoulder height adjuster.
    • Remove the bolt.
    • Rotate the safety belt shoulder height adjuster to detach the lower tab from the B-pillar and remove.
    • To install, tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).

  1. NOTE: Before installation, make sure the safety belt webbing is not twisted and the safety belts and buckles are accessible to the occupants.

    To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. Check the active restraint system for correct operation. For additional information, refer to the appropriate Functional Test procedure in Safety Belt System in this section.
