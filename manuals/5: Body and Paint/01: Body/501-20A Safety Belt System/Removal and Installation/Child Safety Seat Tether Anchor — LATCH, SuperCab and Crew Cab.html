501-20A Safety Belt System 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Child Safety Seat Tether Anchor — LATCH, SuperCab and Crew Cab

Removal and Installation

  1. The lower anchors and tethers for children are serviced as part of the rear seat frames and are not serviceable separately.
    • For Crew Cab, install a new rear seat cushion frame. For additional information, refer to Seat Cushion — Rear, Crew Cab in the Disassembly and Assembly portion of Section  501-10.
    • For SuperCab, install a new rear seat frame. For additional information, refer to Seat Cushion — Rear, SuperCab in the Disassembly and Assembly portion of Section  501-10.
