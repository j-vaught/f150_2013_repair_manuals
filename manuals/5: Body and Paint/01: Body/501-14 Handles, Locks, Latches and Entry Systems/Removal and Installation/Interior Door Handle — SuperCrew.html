501-14 Handles, Locks, Latches and Entry Systems 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Interior Door Handle — SuperCrew

NOTE: LH side shown, RH similar.


Item Part Number Description
1 18226011822601/ 1822600/ 1822600 Interior door handle (LH/RH)
2 16274071627407/ 1627406/ 1627406 Door trim panel (LH/RH)

Removal and Installation

  1. If equipped, remove the window regulator handle.
    1. Separate the handle cover from the handle.
    1. Remove the screw.
    1. Remove the window regulator handle.
    1. Remove the spacer.
  1. Remove the rear door garnish moulding.
    1. Pull outward to release the rear door garnish moulding clips from the door.
    1. Remove the upper rear door trim panel screw.
  1. Remove the rear door handle cover plate.
  1. Remove the rear door handle screw.
  1. Remove the 2 rear door trim panel screws.

  1. NOTICE: Do not pull directly outward on the rear door trim panel. Damage may occur.

    Position the rear door trim panel outward.
    • Lift upward to remove the rear door trim panel and position outward.
    • Disconnect the interior handle activating cable from the interior door handle.
  1. Release the interior door handle retaining tabs and remove the interior door handle.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
