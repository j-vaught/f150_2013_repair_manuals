501-14 Handles, Locks, Latches and Entry Systems 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Ignition Lock Cylinder — Non Functional

Removal

  1. Remove the Passive Anti-Theft System (PATS) transceiver. For additional information, refer to Section  419-01B.
  1. Remove the ignition lock cylinder.
    • Use a 1/8-inch diameter drill bit to drill out the lock cylinder retaining pin.
    • Remove and discard the ignition lock cylinder and drill shavings from the steering column.
  1. Remove the bearing retainer.
  1. Remove the steering column lock housing bearing and steering column lock gear.

Installation

  1. Thoroughly clean all drill shavings from the steering column and inspect it for damage.
  1. Install a new ignition lock cylinder.
    • Verify the ignition lock cylinder operation.
  1. If needed, program the new PATS keys. For additional information, refer to Integrated Keyhead Transmitter (IKT) Key Programming Using Diagnostic Equipment in Section  419-01B.
