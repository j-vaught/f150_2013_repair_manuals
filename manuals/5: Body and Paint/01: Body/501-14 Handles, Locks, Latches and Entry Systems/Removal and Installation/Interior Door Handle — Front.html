501-14 Handles, Locks, Latches and Entry Systems 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Interior Door Handle — Front

NOTE: LH side shown, RH similar.


Item Part Number Description
1 15226011522601/ 1522600/ 1522600 Interior door handle (LH/RH)
2 18239431823943/ 1823942/ 1823942 Door trim panel (LH/RH)

Removal and Installation

  1. If equipped, remove the window regulator handle.
    1. Separate the handle cover from the handle.
    1. Remove the screw.
    1. Remove the window regulator handle.
    1. Remove the spacer.
  1. Remove the 2 front door trim panel lower screws.
  1. Remove the door handle screw cover and screw.
  1. Remove the pull cup screw cover and screw.

  1. NOTICE: Do not pull directly outward on the front door trim panel, as damage may occur.

    Position the front door trim panel outward.
    • Lift upward to remove the front door trim panel and position outward.
    • Disconnect the interior handle activating cable from the interior door handle.
  1. Release the interior door handle retaining tabs and remove the interior door handle.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
