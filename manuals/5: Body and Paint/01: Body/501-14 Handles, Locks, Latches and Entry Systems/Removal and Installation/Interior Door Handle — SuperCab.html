501-14 Handles, Locks, Latches and Entry Systems 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Interior Door Handle — SuperCab

NOTE: LH side shown, RH similar.


Item Part Number Description
1 18226011822601/ 1822600/ 1822600 Interior door handle (LH/RH)

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the rear door trim panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Disconnect the upper door latch release cable from the interior door handle.
  1. Disconnect the lower door latch release cable from the interior door handle, and remove the interior door handle.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
