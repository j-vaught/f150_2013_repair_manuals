Door Ajar Switch — Rear, SuperCab
Item Part Number Description
1 18265B2918265B29 Door ajar switch
2 18264A0118264A01/ 18264A00/ 18264A00 Lower door latch (LH/RH)

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the lower rear door latch. For additional information, refer to Rear Door Latch — Lower, SuperCab in this section.
  1. Insert a screwdriver or suitable tool into the opening on the door latch and push up on the tab.
  1. Rotate the door ajar switch and remove from the door latch.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
