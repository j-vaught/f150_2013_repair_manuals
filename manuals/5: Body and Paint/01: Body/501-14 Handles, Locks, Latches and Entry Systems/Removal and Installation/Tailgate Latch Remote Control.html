501-14 Handles, Locks, Latches and Entry Systems 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Tailgate Latch Remote Control

Removal and Installation

  1. If equipped, remove the tailgate step assist handle. For additional information, refer to Section  501-04.
  1. Remove the bolts and the tailgate access panel (7 bolts for flareside bed, 8 for without step tailgate, or 13 with step tailgate).
    • To install, tighten to 5 Nm (44 lb-in).
  1. Open the 2 clips and release the 2 tailgate latch actuating rods from the tailgate remote control.
  1. If equipped, release the tailgate latch actuating rod locator from the tailgate.
  1. Remove the 2 tailgate release handle nuts and the tailgate remote control.
    • To install, tighten to 9 Nm (80 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
