501-14 Handles, Locks, Latches and Entry Systems 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Door Lock Control Switch

NOTE: LH side shown, RH side similar.


Item Part Number Description
1 1452714527/ 14528/ 14528 Switch plate cover (LH/RH)
2 — Door lock switch electrical connector
3 1402814028 Door lock control switch

Removal and Installation

  1. Lift up to remove the switch plate cover.
  1. Disconnect the electrical connector.
  1. Release the door lock control switch tabs and remove the door lock control switch.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
