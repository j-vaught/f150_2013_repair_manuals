501-14 Handles, Locks, Latches and Entry Systems 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 04/26/2013

Rear Door Latch — SuperCrew

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the rear window regulator and motor. For additional information, refer to Section  501-11.
  1. Disconnect the rear door latch electrical connector.
  1. Disconnect the 2 rear inside door handle cable routing clips from the door.
  1. Open the clip and disconnect the exterior rear door handle actuating rod from the latch.
  1. Remove the 3 door latch bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
  1. Remove the latch.

Installation

  1. NOTICE: Ensure the latch lever is in the relaxed (all the way up) position prior to connecting the exterior rear door handle actuating rod to the latch. Do not apply downward pressure when connecting the exterior rear door handle actuating rod to the latch this could result in improper operation of the power door lock.

    To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. Check for proper operation.
    1. Lock the doors using the power door lock switch.
    1. Attempt to open the door using the exterior door handle.
    1. If the door opens, locate the clip on the latch and re-adjust the position of the clip on the door handle actuating rod.
