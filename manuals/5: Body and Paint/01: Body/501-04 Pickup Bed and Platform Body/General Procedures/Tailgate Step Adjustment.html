501-04 Pickup Bed and Platform Body 2013 F-150
General Procedures Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Tailgate Step Adjustment

  1. Loosen the 4 tailgate step bolts.
  1. Loosen the driver side tailgate step nab pin nut.
  1. Cycle the tailgate step to the fully extended position 2 times.
  1. Using a suitable trim tool or wedge, pry upward on the driver side of the flex step.
  1. Tighten the driver side tailgate step nab pin to 11 Nm (97 lb-in).
  1. Tighten the 4 tailgate step bolts to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).

  1. NOTE: Make sure the tailgate step does not pinch against the tailgate moulding.

    Inspect the tailgate step gap margin and flushness and adjust as necessary.
    1. Gap Margin = 4.0 mm (0.157 in) ± 2.0 mm (0.078 in).
    1. Flushness = 1.0 mm (0.039 in) ± 3.0 mm (0.118 in).
      • Cycle the tailgate step to the fully extended position to make sure of normal operation.
