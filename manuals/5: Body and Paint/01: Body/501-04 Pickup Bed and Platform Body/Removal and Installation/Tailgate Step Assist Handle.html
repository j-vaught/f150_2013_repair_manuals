501-04 Pickup Bed and Platform Body 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Tailgate Step Assist Handle


Item Part Number Description
1 99402B6499402B64 Tailgate assist handle bolt
2 99433009943300 Tailgate assist handle

  1. Lower the tailgate.
  1. Remove the T40 Torx head bolt from the assist handle.
    • To install, tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the assist handle.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
