Removal and Installation

NOTE: Select parts are available for repair of some individual tailgate step components.

  1. Lower the tailgate and open the assist step.
  1. Remove the 2 tailgate step inside bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the 4 tailgate step upper bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the 2 tailgate step nab pin nuts.
    • To install, tighten to 11 Nm (97 lb-in).
  1. Remove the tailgate step.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Adjust as necessary. For additional information, refer to Tailgate Step Adjustment in this section.
