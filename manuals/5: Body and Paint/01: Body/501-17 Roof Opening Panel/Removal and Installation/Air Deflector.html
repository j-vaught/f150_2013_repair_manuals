501-17 Roof Opening Panel 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Air Deflector

Removal and Installation

  1. Place the roof opening panel glass in the fully OPEN position.
  1. Remove the 2 air deflector screws.
  1. Rotate the front of the air deflector up, and slide the pivot points toward the rear of the vehicle to release the air deflector from the retaining hooks.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
