501-17 Roof Opening Panel 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Roof Opening Panel Control Switch

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the overhead console. For additional information, refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Disconnect the 2 overhead console electrical connectors.
  1. Remove the light assembly from the overhead console.
    1. Carefully pull the 2 rear tabs outward and rotate the light assembly slightly away from the overhead console.
    1. Carefully pull the 2 front tabs outward and remove the light assembly from the overhead console.
  1. Disengage the 4 tabs and remove the switch from the light assembly.
  1. Disengage the 2 tabs and remove the switch bezel from the switch.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
