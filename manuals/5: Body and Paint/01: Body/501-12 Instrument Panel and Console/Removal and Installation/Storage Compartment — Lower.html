501-12 Instrument Panel and Console 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Storage Compartment — Lower

Removal and Installation

All vehicles

  1. Remove the lower instrument panel center finish panel. For additional information, refer to Instrument Panel Finish Panel — Center, Lower in this section.

Vehicles equipped with a storage compartment

  1. Remove the 4 instrument panel lower storage compartment screws.
  1. Remove the instrument panel lower storage compartment.

Vehicles equipped with an media bin

  1. Remove the 4 media bin screws.
  1. Remove the media bin.

All vehicles

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
