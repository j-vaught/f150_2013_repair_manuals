501-12 Instrument Panel and Console 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 07/13/2016

Power Point


Special Tool(s)
Remover, Power Point
501-194 or equivalent

Removal

NOTE: Power point cover may differ depending on location.

  1. Open the power point cover.
  1. Position the Power Point Socket Remover so that it engages in the adjacent slot.
    1. Install the receiver cup.
    1. Install and tighten the wing nut.
  1. Tighten the wing nut to pull the power point socket out of the retainer.
  1. Disconnect the electrical connector.

Installation

  1. Connect the electrical connector and push the new power point socket into the retainer.
