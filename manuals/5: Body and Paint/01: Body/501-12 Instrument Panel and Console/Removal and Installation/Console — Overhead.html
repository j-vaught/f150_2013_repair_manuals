Removal and Installation

  1. Pull the rear edge of the overhead console downward, at the locations shown, and then pull rearward.
  1. Disconnect the electrical connectors.
  1. Remove the overhead console.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Make sure to align the overhead console retaining clips to the adjacent retaining clip holes.
