501-03 Body Closures 2013 F-150
General Procedures Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Door Alignment — Rear, SuperCrew

All adjustments

  1. Remove the rear door striker.

Horizontal and vertical adjustments

  1. Loosen the rear door hinge-to-rear door bolts.
  1. Adjust the rear door. For additional information, refer to the dimensions in Section  501-35.
  1. Tighten the rear door hinge-to-rear door bolts to 30 Nm (22 lb-ft).

Fore and aft adjustment

  1. Remove the B-pillar trim panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Loosen the rear door hinge-to-body nuts.
  1. Adjust the door. For additional information, refer to the dimensions in Section  501-35.
  1. Tighten the rear door hinge-to-body nuts to 30 Nm (22 lb-ft).
  1. Install the B-pillar trim panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.

All adjustments

  1. Install the rear door striker.
    • Tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
