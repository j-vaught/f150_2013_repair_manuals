Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the door trim panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Remove the rear door check arm-to-body screw.
    • To install, tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the rear door check arm-to-door nuts.
    • To install, tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
  1. Remove the rear door check arm.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
