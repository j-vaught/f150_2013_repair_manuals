501-16 Wipers and Washers 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Wiper Pivot Arm

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the wiper pivot arm.
    1. Position the wiper pivot arm upward.

    1. NOTE: During installation, it is necessary to pull the locking tab out to its full open (unlatched) position and hold while assembling the pivot arm onto the mounting arm and pivot shaft.

      Pull outward on the retaining tab and remove the wiper pivot arm.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • If necessary, align the wiper blade and pivot arm. For additional information, refer to Wiper Blade and Pivot Arm Adjustment in this section.
