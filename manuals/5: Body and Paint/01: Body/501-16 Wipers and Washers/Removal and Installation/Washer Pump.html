Removal and Installation

WARNING: Carefully read cautionary information on product label. For emergency medical information seek medical advice. In the USA or Canada on Ford/Motorcraft products call the Poison Control Center at: 1-800-959-3673. For additional information, consult the product Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) if available. Failure to follow these instructions may result in serious personal injury.

NOTICE: When servicing the washer pump, be careful not to damage the washer pump seal.

NOTICE: Do not operate the washer pump before filling the washer reservoir. Failure to do so may result in premature washer pump failure.

  1. Remove the RH headlamp assembly. For additional information, refer to Section  417-01.
  1. Drain the windshield washer reservoir.
  1. Disconnect the washer reservoir hose.
  1. Disconnect the washer pump electrical connector.
  1. Remove the washer pump.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
