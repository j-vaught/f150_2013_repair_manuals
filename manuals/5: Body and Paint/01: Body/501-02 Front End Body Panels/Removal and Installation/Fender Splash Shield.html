501-02 Front End Body Panels 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Fender Splash Shield

Removal and Installation

Both splash shields

  1. If equipped, remove the fender moulding. For additional information, refer to Section  501-08.
  1. Remove the wheel and tire. For additional information, refer to Section  204-04

LH splash shield

  1. Open the hood and remove the 5 wiring harness pushpins attached to the splash shield in the engine compartment.
  1. Remove the 3 splash shield pushpins and the 5 splash shield screws.
  1. Partially lower the splash shield and remove the 2 air box deflector pushpins from the engine side of the splash shield.

RH splash shield

  1. Open the hood and remove the 4 wiring harness pushpins attached to the splash shield in the engine compartment.
  1. Remove the 3 splash shield pushpins and the 5 splash shield screws.

Both splash shields

  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
