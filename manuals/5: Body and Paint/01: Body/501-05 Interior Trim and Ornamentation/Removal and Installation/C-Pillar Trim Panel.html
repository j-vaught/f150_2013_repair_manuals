501-05 Interior Trim and Ornamentation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

C-Pillar Trim Panel

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the rear seat assembly. For additional information, refer to Section  501-10.
  1. Using a non-marring flat-blade tool, remove the C-pillar screw cover.
  1. Remove the C-pillar screw.
  1. Remove the C-pillar pin-type retainer.
  1. Reposition the C-pillar trim.
  1. Remove the safety belt anchor bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).
  1. Reposition the safety belt through the trim panel.
  1. Remove C-pillar trim panel.

  1. NOTE: The C-pillar trim panel has locator pins that must be positioned correctly for installation.

    NOTE: The C-pillar trim panel should be parallel to the rear window blackout.

    To install, reverse removal procedure.
