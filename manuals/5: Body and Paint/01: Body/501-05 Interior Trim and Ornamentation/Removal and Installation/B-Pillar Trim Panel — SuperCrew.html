501-05 Interior Trim and Ornamentation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

B-Pillar Trim Panel — SuperCrew

Removal and Installation

  1. Position seats fully down and forward.
  1. Depower the Supplemental Restraint System (SRS) . For additional information, refer to Section  501-20B.
  1. Pull upward to release the scuff plate clips and remove the front door scuff plate.
    • Detach the scuff plate from the weatherstrip.
  1. Position the front and rear weatherstrips aside.
  1. Remove the safety belt D-ring trim cover.
  1. Remove the safety belt D-ring bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).

  1. NOTE: Starting at the top of the cover, use a non-marring tool.

    Remove the B-pillar bolt cover.
  1. Remove the B-pillar bolt.
  1. Remove the safety belt anchor bolt trim cover.
  1. Remove the safety belt anchor bolt.
    • Disconnect electrical connector.
    • To install, tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).
  1. Pull upward to release the scuff plate clips and remove the rear door scuff plate.
    • Detach the scuff plate from the weatherstrip clip.
  1. Remove the safety belt trim ring and pull the safety belt through the B-pillar.
  1. Remove the B-pillar trim panel.

  1. NOTE: The B-pillar trim panel has a 2-way locator and a 4-way locator that must be positioned correctly on installation.

    To install, reverse the removal procedure.
