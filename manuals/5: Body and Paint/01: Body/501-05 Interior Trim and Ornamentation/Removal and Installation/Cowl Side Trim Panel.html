501-05 Interior Trim and Ornamentation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Cowl Side Trim Panel

Removal and Installation

Both sides

  1. Pull upward to release the scuff plate clips and remove the front door scuff plate.
    • Detach the scuff plate from the weatherstrip.

LH side

  1. Remove the cowl trim panel.

RH side

  1. Remove the fuse panel access door.
  1. Remove the pin-type retainer from the cowl trim panel.

Both sides

  1. Remove the cowl trim panel.

  1. NOTE: Make sure the cowl side trim panel J-hook is correctly installed.

    To install, reverse the removal procedure.
