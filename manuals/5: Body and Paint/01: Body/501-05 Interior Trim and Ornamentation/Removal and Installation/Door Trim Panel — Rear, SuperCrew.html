Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the rear door garnish moulding.
    • Pull outward to release the rear door garnish moulding clips from the door.
  1. Remove the window control switch panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-11.
  1. Remove the rear door handle cover plate.
  1. Remove the rear door handle screw.
  1. Remove the 3 rear door trim panel screws.

  1. NOTICE: Do not pull directly outward on the rear door trim panel. Damage may occur.

    Remove the rear door trim panel.
    • Lift upward to remove the rear door trim panel and position aside.
    • Disconnect the interior handle activating cable from the interior door handle and remove the rear door trim panel.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
