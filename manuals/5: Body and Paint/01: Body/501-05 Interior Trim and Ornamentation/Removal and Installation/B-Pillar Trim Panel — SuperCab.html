501-05 Interior Trim and Ornamentation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

B-Pillar Trim Panel — SuperCab

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the rear seat backrest. For additional information, refer to Section  501-10.
  1. Pull upward to release the scuff plate clips and remove the scuff plate trim panel.
    • Detach the scuff plate from the weatherstrip.
  1. Reposition the rear door weatherstrip.
  1. Remove the B-pillar trim panel pin-type retainer.
  1. Remove the B-pillar trim panel bolt cover.

  1. NOTE: Starting at the top of the cover, use a non-marring tool.

    Remove the B-pillar trim panel bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 9 Nm (80 lb-in).
  1. Pull inward to release the B-pillar trim panel clips.
  1. Reposition the B-pillar trim panel.
  1. Remove the safety belt anchor bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).
  1. Reposition the safety belt through the B-pillar trim panel and remove the B-pillar trim panel.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
