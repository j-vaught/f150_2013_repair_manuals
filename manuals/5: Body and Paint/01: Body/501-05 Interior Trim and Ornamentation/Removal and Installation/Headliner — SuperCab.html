Removal

  1. Remove the A-pillar trim panel. For additional information, refer to A-Pillar Trim Panel in this section.
  1. Reposition the B-pillar trim panels. For additional information, refer to B-Pillar Trim Panel — SuperCab in this section.
  1. Remove the driver seat headrest.
  1. Open the garment hook screw covers.
  1. Remove the garment hook screws and remove the garment hooks.
  1. Remove the overhead console. For additional information, refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Remove the LH and RH sun visors.
    • Remove the sun visor retainer screws.
    • Reposition headliner and disconnect electrical connector.
  1. Remove the sun visor clip screws and remove the sun visor clips.
  1. Remove the rear door latch cover screws and remove the rear door latch covers.
  1. Remove the headliner pin-type retainers.
  1. If equipped, disconnect the mirror electrical connector and the rain sensor.
  1. Disconnect the headliner electrical connectors.
  1. Rotate the front of the headliner towards the driver door.
  1. Remove the headliner through the driver door.

Installation

  1. Position the headliner in the vehicle so that the sun visor and coat hook openings are aligned.
  1. Install the LH and RH sun visors.
    • Reposition headliner and connect electrical connector.
    • Install the sun visor retainer screws.
  1. Install the sun visor clip and sun visor clip screws.
  1. Install the garment hooks and garment hook screws.
  1. Close the garment hook screw covers.
  1. If equipped with a roof opening panel, secure the hook-and-loop tape around the front edge of the roof opening panel and then secure the back edge of the roof opening panel so that it is flush and the headliner does not sag. If sagging occurs, loosen the sun visors and coat hooks to realign the headliner. Retighten the sunvisors and coat hooks.
  1. Install the headliner pin-type retainers.
  1. Connect the headliner electrical connectors.
  1. If equipped, connect the mirror electrical connector and the rain sensor.
  1. Install the overhead console. For additional information, refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Install the rear door latch covers and install the rear door latch cover screws.
  1. Install the driver seat headrest.
  1. Install the B-pillar trim panels. For additional information, refer to B-Pillar Trim Panel — SuperCab in this section.
  1. Install the A-pillar trim panel. For additional information, refer to A-Pillar Trim Panel in this section.
