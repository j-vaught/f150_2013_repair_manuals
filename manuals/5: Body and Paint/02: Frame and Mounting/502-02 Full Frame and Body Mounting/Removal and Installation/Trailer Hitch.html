502-02 Full Frame and Body Mounting 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Trailer Hitch

Removal and Installation
Item Part Number Description
1 — Trailer light/brake electrical connector
2 N811645N811645-S-S Trailer hitch bolts
3 W504042W504042-S-S Trailer hitch

  1. Remove the rear bumper. For additional information, refer to Section  501-19.
  1. Disconnect the trailer light/brake electrical connector.
  1. Remove the trailer hitch bolts and remove the trailer hitch.
    • To install, tighten to 175 Nm (129 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
