502-02 Full Frame and Body Mounting 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 11/29/2012

Transmission Support Crossmember


Item Part Number Description
1 W520114W520114 Transmission support crossmember nuts (4 required)
2 W714418W714418 Transmission support crossmember bolts (4 required)
3 W520114W520114 Transmission mount nuts (2 required)
4 7H4747H474 Transmission support crossmember

Removal and Installation

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information, refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Remove the 2 heat shield screws.
  1. Support the transmission with a suitable jack.
  1. Remove the 2 transmission support nuts.
  1. Remove the 4 transmission support crossmember nuts.
  1. Remove the 4 transmission support crossmember support bolts.
  1. Remove the transmission support crossmember.

Installation

  1. Position the transmission support crossmember and install the transmission support crossmember support nuts and bolts.
    • Tighten the nuts to 103 Nm (76 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the transmission jack.
  1. Install the 2 transmission support nuts.
    • Tighten to 103 Nm (76 lb-ft).
  1. Install the 2 heat shield screws.
    • Tighten to 20 Nm (177 lb-in).
