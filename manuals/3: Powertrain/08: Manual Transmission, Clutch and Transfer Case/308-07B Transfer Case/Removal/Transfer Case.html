  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. Refer to Section  100-02.
  1. If equipped, remove the 4 skid plate bolts and remove the skid plate.
  1. Drain the fluid if the transfer case is to be disassembled.
    • Apply thread sealant to the drain plug threads and install the drain plug when finished draining.
      • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. Remove the front driveshaft. Refer to Section  205-01.
  1. Remove the rear driveshaft. Refer to Section  205-01.
  1. Disconnect the shift motor electrical connector and detach the 2 harness retainer clips from the transfer case.
  1. Disconnect the vent hose from the transfer case.
  1. Remove the RH exhaust heat shield bolt from the transmission support crossmember.
  1. Remove the LH exhaust heat shield bolt from the transmission support crossmember.
  1. Support the front of the transmission.
  1. Remove and discard the 4 transmission support crossmember nuts and bolts.
  1. Remove the 2 transmission mount nuts and the transmission support crossmember.
  1. Remove the bolt from the LH exhaust support bracket.
  1. Remove the 4 transmission mount bolts and the transmission mount.
  1. Using a transmission jack, position it to the transfer case. Secure the transfer case to the jack with a safety strap.
  1. Remove and discard the 9 transfer case-to-transmission bolts.
  1. Separate the transfer case from the transmission and move the transfer case rearward 25.4 mm (1 in). Move the transfer case rearward off the output shaft and lower it from the vehicle.
