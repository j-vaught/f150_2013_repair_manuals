Transfer Case — Right Side

  1. Position the transfer case to the transmission and onto the output shaft. With the transfer case 25.4 mm (1 in) from the transmission, push the transfer case to the transmission.

  1. NOTE: Do not reuse the old transfer case-to-transmission bolts.

    Install 9 new transfer case-to-transmission bolts.
    • Tighten the bolts evenly in a cross pattern to 17 Nm (150 lb-in).
  1. Install the transmission mount and the 4 transmission mount bolts.
    • Tighten to 90 Nm (66 lb-ft).
  1. Install the LH exhaust support bracket and bolt.
    • Tighten to 35 Nm (26 lb-ft).
  1. Position the transmission support crossmember and the 2 transmission mount nuts.
    • Do not tighten at this time.
  1. Install the 4 new transmission support crossmember bolts and nuts, then tighten the transmission support crossmember and transmission mount nuts.
    1. Install the transmission support crossmember bolts.
      • Remove the transmission jack from under the transmission pan rail.
    1. Install the transmission support crossmember nuts and tighten to 90 Nm (66 lb-ft).
    1. Tighten the transmission mount nuts to 103 Nm (76 lb-ft).
  1. Install the LH exhaust heat shield bolt to the transmission support crossmember.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. Install the RH exhaust heat shield bolt to the transmission support crossmember.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. Connect the vent hose to the transfer case.
  1. Connect the shift motor electrical connector and attach the 2 harness retainer clips to the transfer case.

  1. NOTE: Align the index marks made during removal.

    Install the rear driveshaft. Refer to Section  205-01.

  1. NOTE: Align the index marks made during removal.

    Install the front driveshaft. Refer to Section  205-01.
  1. Fill the transfer case. Refer to Transfer Case Draining and Filling.
  1. If equipped, position the skid plate and install the 4 skid plate bolts.
    • Tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).
