310-02 Acceleration Control 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Accelerator Pedal — Adjustable
Item Part Number Description
1 — Accelerator pedal-to-brake pedal cable (part of 9F836)
2 — Accelerator Pedal Position (APP) sensor electrical connector (part of 12A581)
3 — APP sensor electrical connector wiring harness retainer (part of 12A581)
4 9F8369F836 Accelerator pedal assembly
5 W713384W713384 Accelerator pedal assembly bolts (3 required)

Removal

  1. If possible, adjust the pedals to the full forward (away from driver) position.

  1. NOTE: To prevent setting DTCs, make sure the ignition switch is in the OFF position prior to disconnecting the accelerator pedal electrical connector.

    Disconnect the Accelerator Pedal Position (APP) sensor electrical connector.
  1. Disconnect the accelerator pedal-to-brake pedal cable from the brake pedal.
  1. Remove the 3 bolts and the accelerator pedal assembly.

Installation

  1. Install the accelerator pedal assembly and the 3 bolts.
    • Tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. Connect the accelerator pedal-to-brake pedal cable to the brake pedal.
  1. Connect the APP sensor electrical connector.
  1. Carry out the adjustable pedal indexing procedure. For additional information, refer to Section  206-06.
