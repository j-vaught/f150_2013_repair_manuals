309-00 Exhaust System 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Exhaust Intermediate Pipe

Removal

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information, refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Remove the muffler and tail pipe. For additional information, refer to Muffler and Tailpipe in this section.
  1. Remove the 2 exhaust Y-pipe dual catalytic converter-to-exhaust intermediate pipe bolts and remove the exhaust intermediate pipe from the vehicle.

Installation

  1. NOTE: Make sure the exhaust intermediate pipe is installed so the rear L-notch nub is at the 1 o'clock position.

    Position the exhaust intermediate pipe into the vehicle and install the 2 exhaust Y-pipe dual catalytic converter-to-exhaust intermediate pipe bolts and tighten in the following sequence.
    1. Snug the outer exhaust Y-pipe dual catalytic converter-to-exhaust intermediate pipe bolt.
    1. Tighten the inner exhaust Y-pipe dual catalytic converter-to-exhaust intermediate pipe bolt to 63 Nm (46 lb-ft).
    1. Tighten the outer exhaust Y-pipe dual catalytic converter-to-exhaust intermediate pipe bolt to 63 Nm (46 lb-ft).
  1. Install the muffler and tail pipe. For additional information, refer to Muffler and Tailpipe in this section.
