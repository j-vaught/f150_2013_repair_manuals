307-01 Automatic Transaxle/Transmission — 6R80 2013 F-150
In-vehicle Repair Procedure revision date: 11/01/2013

Manual Control Lever Shaft and Seal


Special Tool(s)
Installer, Shifter Fluid Seal

Material
Item Specification

Removal

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. Refer to Section  100-02.
  1. If equipped, remove the nut and the selector lever cable splash shield.

  1. NOTICE: To prevent selector lever cable damage, do not apply force to the selector lever cable between the manual control lever and the selector lever cable bracket.

    NOTE: The column shift is shown, the floor shift is similar.

    Disconnect the transmission selector lever cable end from the manual control lever.
  1. Remove the main control. Refer to Main Control.
  1. Remove the park rod actuating plate.
  1. Remove the manual control lever.
    1. Remove the roll pin.
    1. Slide the manual control lever shaft out of the case.
    1. Hold the spacer to keep it from falling out of the case.
    1. Remove the manual valve inner lever and the park rod as an assembly.
  1. Remove and discard the manual control lever seal.

Installation

  1. Position a new manual control lever seal in place.
  1. Using the Shifter Fluid Seal Installer, install a new manual control lever shaft seal.
  1. Install the manual control lever in the case.
    1. Slide the manual control lever in the case.
    1. Position the spacer in place while sliding the manual control lever into the spacer.
    1. Position the manual valve inner lever while sliding the manual control lever and lining up the roll pin hole.
    1. Install the roll pin.
  1. Position the park rod in place. Push down on the park pawl and position the park rod in place.
  1. While holding the park rod down, install the park rod actuating plate.
    • Tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
  1. Install the main control. Refer to Main Control.

  1. NOTICE: To prevent selector lever cable damage, do not apply force to the selector lever cable between the manual control lever and the selector lever cable bracket.

    NOTE: The column shift is shown, the floor shift is similar.

    Install the selector lever cable end on the manual control lever.
  1. If equipped, install the selector lever cable splash shield and the nut.
  1. Fill the transmission with clean transmission fluid. Refer to Transmission Fluid Drain and Refill.
