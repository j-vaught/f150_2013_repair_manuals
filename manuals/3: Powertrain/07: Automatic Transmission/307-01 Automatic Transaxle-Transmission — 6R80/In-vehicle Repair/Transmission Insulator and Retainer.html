Removal

All vehicles

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. Refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Remove the RH exhaust heat shield bolt from the transmission support crossmember.
  1. Remove the LH exhaust heat shield bolt from the transmission support crossmember.

RWD vehicles

  1. Remove the 2 transmission insulator and retainer nuts.
  1. Remove the LH isolator cap bolt.
  1. Position a high-lift transmission jack under the transmission.
  1. Remove and discard the 4 transmission support crossmember nuts and bolts (2 each side) and remove the transmission support crossmember.
  1. Remove the 3 transmission insulator and retainer bolts and remove the transmission insulator and retainer.

4WD vehicles

  1. If equipped, remove the 4 skid plate bolts and remove the skid plate.
  1. Remove the 2 transmission insulator and retainer nuts.
  1. Remove the LH insolator cap bolt.

  1. NOTE: Right side shown, left side similar. Transmission crossmember removed for clarity.

    Remove the 4 transmission insulator and retainer bolts.
  1. Support the transfer case.
  1. Remove and discard the 4 transmission support crossmember nuts and bolts.
  1. Remove the transmission insulator and retainer.

Installation

RWD vehicles

  1. Install the transmission insulator and retainer and the 3 bolts.
    • Tighten to 90 Nm (66 lb-ft).
  1. Install the transmission support crossmember and the 4 new transmission support crossmember nuts and bolts (2 each side).
    • Tighten to 90 Nm (66 lb-ft).
  1. Lower the transmission onto the transmission support crossmember and install the 2 transmission insulator and retainer nuts.
    • Tighten to 103 Nm (75 lb-ft).
  1. Install the LH isolator cap bolt.
    • Tighten to 35 Nm (26 lb-ft).

4WD vehicles

  1. Loosely install the transmission insulator and retainer and the 4 bolts.
  1. Position the transmission support crossmember and loosely install the 2 transmission insulator and retainer nuts.
  1. Install the 4 new transmission support crossmember bolts and nuts.
    • Tighten the transmission insulator and retainer bolts to 90 Nm (66 lb-ft).
    • Tighten the transmission support crossmember bolts and nuts to 90 Nm (66 lb-ft).
    • Tighten the transmission insulator and retainer nuts to 103 Nm (75 lb-ft).
  1. Install the bolt for the LH isolator cap bolt.
    • Tighten to 35 Nm (26 lb-ft).

All vehicles

  1. Install the LH exhaust heat shield bolt to the transmission support crossmember.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. Install the RH exhaust heat shield bolt to the transmission support crossmember.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).

4WD vehicles

  1. If equipped, install the skid plate and the 4 skid plate bolts.
    • Tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).
