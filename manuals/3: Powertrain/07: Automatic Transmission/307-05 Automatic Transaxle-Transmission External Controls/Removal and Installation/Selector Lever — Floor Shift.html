307-05 Automatic Transaxle/Transmission External Controls 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Selector Lever — Floor Shift

Selector Lever — Floor Shift


Item Part Number Description
1 — Console upper trim panel
2 7E3957E395 Selector lever cable
3 72107210 Selector lever assembly
4 N606678N606678-S-S Selector lever assembly bolts (4 required)
5 72137213 Selector lever knob

Removal

  1. Remove the instrument panel to floor console finish panel.
  1. Remove the floor console upper finish panel.
    • Starting at the rear of the upper finish panel, carefully pry up the upper finish panel and disconnect the electrical connectors.

  1. NOTICE: Do not pry on selector lever cable or the selector lever pin may be damaged.

    Disconnect the selector lever cable end from the selector lever pin by gently pulling the selector lever cable end straight off the selector lever arm.

  1. NOTE: The selector lever bezel has been removed for clarity. Do not remove the selector lever bezel.

    Remove the 4 selector lever assembly bolts.
  1. Disconnect the selector lever cable from the selector lever cable bracket.
  1. Disconnect the selector lever harness connector and remove the selector lever from the vehicle.

Installation

  1. NOTE: When installing the selector lever cable, make sure that the selector lever cable locking tabs are locked in place. Press the selector lever cable into the bracket and listen for the selector lever cable to click in place. Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure that it is locked into the bracket.

    Install the selector lever cable in the selector lever cable bracket.
  1. Position the selector lever assembly in the console and install the 4 bolts.
    • Tighten to 8 Nm (71 lb-in).
  1. Connect the selector lever harness connector.
  1. Connect the selector lever cable onto the selector lever arm.
    • Listen for the audible click.
    • Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure that it is installed correctly.
  1. Connect and install the floor console upper finish panel.
  1. Install the instrument panel to floor console finish panel.
  1. Adjust the selector lever cable. For additional information, refer to Selector Lever Cable Adjustment — Floor Shift in this section.
