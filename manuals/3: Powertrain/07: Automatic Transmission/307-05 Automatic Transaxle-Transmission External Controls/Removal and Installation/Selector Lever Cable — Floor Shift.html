Removal

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information, refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Remove the instrument panel to floor console finish panel.
  1. Remove the floor console upper finish panel.
    • Starting at the rear of the upper finish panel, carefully pry up the upper finish panel and disconnect the electrical connectors.

  1. NOTICE: Do not pry on selector lever cable or the selector lever pin may be damaged.

    Disconnect the selector lever cable end from the selector lever pin by gently pulling the selector lever cable end straight off the selector lever arm.
  1. Remove the center console LH kick panel.
  1. Disconnect the selector lever cable from the selector lever cable bracket.
  1. Push the rubber grommet and selector lever cable through the floor panel.
  1. Detach the selector lever cable from the routing clip.
  1. If equipped, remove the nut and the selector lever cable splash shield.

  1. NOTE: The 3.5L is shown. The 3.7L, 5.0L and 6.2L do not have a heat shield.

    Clean cable adjustment lock of any debris.

  1. NOTICE: To prevent selector lever cable damage, do not apply force to the selector lever cable between the manual control lever and the selector lever cable bracket.

    Move the locking tab up and disconnect the selector lever cable from the manual control lever.
  1. Remove the selector lever cable from the selector lever cable bracket.

Installation

  1. NOTICE: To prevent selector lever cable damage, do not apply force to the selector lever cable between the manual control lever and the selector lever cable bracket.

    NOTE: When installing the selector lever cable, make sure that the selector lever cable locking tabs are locked in place. Press the selector lever cable into the selector lever cable bracket and listen for the selector lever cable to click into place. Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure that it is locked into the selector lever cable bracket.

    Attach the selector lever cable to the selector lever cable bracket.
  1. Attach the selector lever cable to the routing clip.
  1. Install the rubber grommet and selector lever cable through the floor panel.

  1. NOTE: When installing the selector lever cable, make sure that the selector lever cable locking tabs are locked in place. Press the selector lever cable into the selector lever cable bracket and listen for the selector lever cable to click into place. Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure that it is locked into the selector lever cable bracket.

    Connect the selector lever cable onto the selector lever.
    1. Connect the selector lever cable onto the selector lever cable bracket.
    1. Connect the selector lever cable onto the selector lever arm.
      • Listen for the audible click.
      • Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure that it is installed correctly.
  1. Install the center console LH kick panel.
  1. Connect and install the floor console upper finish panel.
  1. Install the instrument panel to floor console finish panel.
  1. Place the selector lever in DRIVE.
  1. Place the manual control lever in DRIVE.
    1. Rotate the manual lever counterclockwise 3 detents into the manual 1st position.
    1. Rotate the manual lever clockwise 3 detents into Drive.
  1. Connect the selector lever cable onto the manual control lever and move the locking tab down.
    • Listen for the audible click, then push back on the locking tab to verify it is locked.
    • Pull back on the selector lever cable to verify it is installed correctly to the manual lever.
  1. If equipped, install the selector lever cable splash shield and the nut.
    • Tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
  1. Verify the vehicle will start in PARK or NEUTRAL and the backup lamps illuminate in REVERSE.
