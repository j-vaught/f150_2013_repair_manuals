Removal

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. Refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Remove the steering column shrouds. Refer to Section  211-04.
  1. Remove the lower insturment panel trim panel.
  1. Position the selector lever into the D position.

  1. NOTICE: To prevent selector lever cable damage, do not apply force to the selector lever cable assembly between the selector lever mechanism and the steering column bracket.

    Disconnect the selector lever cable from the steering column.
    1. Disconnect the selector lever cable from the selector lever mechanism.
    1. Carefully lift the locking tab and disconnect the selector lever cable from the steering column bracket.
    1. Disconnect the selector lever cable retainer.
  1. Push the rubber grommet and selector lever cable through the floor pan.
  1. Detach the selector lever cable from the routing clip.
  1. If equipped, remove the nut and the selector lever cable splash shield.

  1. NOTICE: To prevent selector lever cable damage, do not apply force to the selector lever cable assembly between the manual control lever and the selector lever cable bracket.

    Disconnect the selector lever cable from the manual control lever.
  1. Remove and discard the selector lever cable retaining clip.
    • Lift the lock tabs on the retaining clip arms to release the clip. Pull the clip and selector lever cable from the selector lever cable bracket.
  1. Remove the selector lever cable.

Installation

  1. Partially install the selector lever cable through the floor opening.
  1. Route the selector lever cable through the instrument panel to the steering column.

  1. NOTE: When installing the selector lever cable, make sure that the selector lever cable locking tabs are locked in place. Press the selector lever cable into the bracket and listen for the cable to click in place. Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure that it is locked into the bracket.

    Connect the selector lever cable onto the steering column.
    1. Connect the selector lever cable retainer.
    1. Connect the selector lever cable onto the steering column bracket.
    1. Connect the selector lever cable onto the selector lever mechanism.
      • Listen for the audible click.
      • Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure that it is correctly installed.
  1. Pull the rubber grommet and selector lever cable up through the floor pan.
  1. Install the lower instrument panel trim panel.
  1. Attach the selector lever cable to the routing clip.

  1. NOTE: When installing the selector lever cable, make sure that the selector lever cable locking tabs are locked in place. Press the selector lever cable into the bracket and listen for the cable to click in place. Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure that it is locked into the bracket.

    Install the selector lever cable into the selector lever cable bracket and install a new retaining clip.
  1. Place the manual control lever in DRIVE.
    1. Rotate the manual lever counterclockwise 3 detents into the manual 1st position.
    1. Rotate the manual lever clockwise 3 detents into Drive.
  1. Unlock the lock tab on the selector lever cable by sliding it up.

  1. NOTE: Apply pressure to the selector lever cable end while installing it onto the manual control lever and make sure the selector lever and manual control lever has not moved out of Drive.

    Install the selector lever cable onto the manual control lever.
    • Listen for the audible click.
    • Pull back on the selector lever cable to make sure it is correctly installed.
  1. Lock the selector lever cable lock tab into place by pressing the lock tab down.
  1. If equipped, install the selector lever cable splash shield and the nut.
    • Tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
  1. Verify the vehicle will start in PARK or NEUTRAL and the backup lamps illuminate in REVERSE.
  1. Place the selector lever to the PARK position and remove the key.
  1. Install the steering column shrouds. Refer to Section  211-04.
