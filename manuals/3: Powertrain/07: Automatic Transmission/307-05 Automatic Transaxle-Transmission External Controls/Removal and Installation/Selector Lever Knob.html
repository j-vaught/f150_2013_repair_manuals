Removal

  1. Remove the instrument panel to floor console finish panel.
  1. Remove the floor console upper finish panel.
    • Starting at the rear of the upper finish panel, carefully pry up the upper finish panel and disconnect the electrical connectors.
  1. Remove and discard the RH selector lever knob cover.
    1. Start at the bottom front.
    1. Move towards the top front and remove the cover.
  1. Remove and discard the LH selector lever knob cover.
    1. Start at the bottom front.
    1. Move towards the top front and remove the cover.
  1. Remove and discard the RH selector lever knob screw.
  1. Remove and discard the LH selector lever knob screw.
  1. Disconnect the SelectShift™ and the Tow/Haul electrical connectors and remove the selector lever knob.

Installation

  1. Connect the SelectShift™ and the Tow/Haul electrical connectors and install the selector lever knob.
  1. Install a new LH selector lever knob screw.
  1. Install a new RH selector lever knob screw.
  1. Install a new LH selector lever knob cover.
  1. Install a new RH selector lever knob cover.
  1. Connect and install the floor console upper finish panel.
  1. Install the instrument panel to floor console finish panel.
