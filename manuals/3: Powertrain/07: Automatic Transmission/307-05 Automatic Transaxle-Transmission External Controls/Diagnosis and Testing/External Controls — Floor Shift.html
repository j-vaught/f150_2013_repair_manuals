307-05 Automatic Transaxle/Transmission External Controls 2013 F-150
Diagnosis and Testing Procedure revision date: 10/13/2016

External Controls — Floor Shift


Special Tool(s)
Fluke 77-IV Digital Multimeter
FLU77-4 or equivalent
Vehicle Communication Module (VCM) and Integrated Diagnostic System (IDS) software with appropriate hardware, or equivalent scan tool

Inspection and Verification

  1. Verify the customer concern by operating the transmission external control.
  1. Visually inspect for obvious signs of mechanical and electrical damage; refer to the following chart:

      Visual Inspection Chart


    Mechanical   Electrical  
    • Damaged Brake Shift Interlock Actuator (BSIA)
    • Damaged Tow/Haul switch
    • Damaged SelectShift™ switch
    • Failed fuse(s):
      • Body Control Module (BCM) 18 (10A)
      • BCM 42 (5A)
    • PCM
    • Loose or damaged connections
    • Damaged wiring harness
    • Corroded or damaged terminals


  1. If the fault is not visually evident, determine the symptom. GO to Symptom Chart — External Controls, Floor Shift or GO to Symptom Chart — NVH Floor Shift.

PINPOINT TEST E : THE BSIA SYSTEM DOES NOT RELEASE/LOCK CORRECTLY
NOTE: Inspect the selector lever cable before proceeding with this pinpoint test.
E1  CHECK THE STOPLAMPS
  • Apply the brake pedal and observe the stoplamps.

Do the stoplamps operate correctly?

Yes GO to E2.
No REFER to Section  417-01.


E2  CHECK THE BSIA POWER CIRCUIT FOR VOLTAGE
  • Disconnect: Floor Shifter C3245.
  • Ignition ON.
  • Measure the voltage between the BSIA C3245 Pin 1, circuit CET53 (BU/OG), harness side and ground.
  • Apply the brake pedal.
  • With the brake pedal applied, measure the voltage between the BSIA C3245 Pin 1, circuit CET53 (BU/OG), harness side and ground.

Is the voltage greater than 10 volts with the brake pedal applied and 0 volts with the brake pedal not applied?

Yes GO to E3.
No VERIFY the BCM fuse 18 (10A) is OK, If OK REPAIR circuit CET53 (BU/OG) for an open. CLEAR the DTC. TEST the system for normal operation. If not OK, REFER to the Wiring Diagrams manual to identify the possible causes of the circuit short.
If voltage was present with the brake pedal not applied, GO to E4.


E3  CHECK THE BSIA GROUND CIRCUIT FOR AN OPEN
  • Ignition OFF.
  • Measure the resistance between the selector lever C3245 Pin 5, circuit GD138 (BK/WH), harness side and ground.

Is the resistance less than 5 ohms?

Yes INSTALL a new selector lever. REFER to Selector Lever — Floor Shift in this section. CLEAR the DTC. TEST the system for normal operation.
No REPAIR circuit GD138 (BK/WH) for an open. CLEAR the DTC. TEST the system for normal operation.


E4  CHECK THE BSIA OUTPUT CIRCUIT FOR A SHORT TO POWER
  • Disconnect: BCM C2280B .
  • Inspect C3245 and C2280B for damaged, pushed-out terminals or loose wires.
  • Ignition ON.
  • Measure the voltage between the selector lever C3245 Pin 1, circuit CET53 (BU/OG), harness side and ground.

Is any voltage present?

Yes REPAIR circuit CET53 (BU/OG) for a short to power. CLEAR the DTC. TEST the system for normal operation.
No GO to E5.


E5  CHECK FOR CORRECT BCM OPERATION
  • Disconnect all BCM connectors.
  • Check for:
    • corrosion
    • damaged pins
    • pushed-out pins
  • Connect all the BCM connectors and make sure they seat correctly.
  • Operate the system and verify the concern is still present.

Is the concern still present?

Yes INSTALL a new BCM. Refer to the appropriate Removal and Installation procedure in Section 419-10. . CLEAR the DTC. TEST the system for normal operation.
No The system is operating correctly at this time. The concern may have been caused by a loose or corroded connector.



PINPOINT TEST F : THE SHIFT CONTROL IS OUT OF CORRECT GEAR RELATIONSHIP
F1  CHECK THE SHIFT CONTROL LINKAGE
  • Ignition ON.
  • Apply the brake pedal.
  • Gain access to the selector lever linkage.
  • Position the selector lever in all ranges.
  • Observe all selector lever linkage during operation.

Is the selector lever linkage damaged?

Yes INSTALL a new selector lever. REFER to Selector Lever — Floor Shift in this section. TEST the system for normal operation.
No GO to F2.


F2  CHECK THE SELECTOR LEVER CABLE
  • Check the selector lever cable and bracket installation and tightness.

Is the selector lever cable correctly installed and adjusted?

Yes VERIFY the selector lever cable adjustment. ADJUST the selector lever cable if necessary. REFER to Selector Lever Cable Adjustment — Floor Shift in this section. GO to F3.
No REPAIR as necessary. TEST the system for normal operation.


F3  CHECK THE LINKAGE/CABLE FOR CORRECT GEAR RELATIONSHIP
  • Apply the brake pedal.
  • Position the selector lever in all ranges.

Does the indicator match the gear selection?

Yes System OK. TEST the system for normal operation.
No REFER to Selector Lever Cable — Floor Shift in this section. TEST the system for normal operation.



PINPOINT TEST G : THE TOW/HAUL SWITCH DOES NOT OPERATE CORRECTLY
NOTE: Vehicles equipped with a 3.7L engine use PCM C175T.
NOTE: Vehicles equipped with a 5.0L or 6.2L engine use PCM C1381T.
NOTE: Vehicles equipped with a 3.5L engine use PCM C1551B.
G1  CHECK DTCS
  • Ignition ON.
  • Connect the scan tool.
  • Start the engine and RUN the Key ON Engine Running (KOER) self-test.

Is DTC P1780 set?

Yes GO to G2.
No No trouble found with the Tow/Haul system. REFER to Section  413-01 if Tow/Haul lamp does not illuminate.


G2  CHECK THE BCM FUSE 42 (5A)
  • Ignition OFF.
  • Remove Body Control Module (BCM) fuse 42 (5A).
  • Check fuse: BCM 42 (5A) .

Is the resistance less than 5 ohms?

Yes GO to G4.
No GO to G3.


G3  CHECK THE TOW/HAUL POWER CIRCUIT FOR A SHORT TO GROUND
  • Disconnect: Selector Lever C3245.
  • Disconnect: BCM C2280B .
  • Inspect C3245 and C2280B for damaged, pushed-out terminals or loose wires.
  • Measure the resistance between the selector lever C3245 Pin 6, circuit CBP42 (GN), harness side and ground.

Is the resistance greater than 10,000 ohms?

Yes GO to G4.
No REPAIR circuit CBP42 (GN) for a short to ground. CLEAR the DTC. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.


G4  CHECK THE TOW/HAUL POWER CIRCUIT FOR VOLTAGE
  • Disconnect: Selector Lever C3245.
  • Inspect C3245 for damaged, pushed-out terminals or loose wires.
  • Ignition ON.
  • Measure the voltage between the selector lever C3245 Pin 6, circuit CBP42 (GN), harness side and ground.

Is the voltage greater than 10 volts?

Yes GO to G5.
No REPAIR circuit CBP42 (GN) for an open. CLEAR the DTC. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.


G5  CHECK THE TOW/HAUL INPUT CIRCUIT FOR A SHORT TO GROUND
  • Disconnect: PCM C175T.
  • Disconnect: PCM C1381T.
  • Disconnect: PCM C1551B.
  • Inspect the connector for damaged, pushed-out terminals, corrosion or loose wires.
  • Measure the resistance between the selector lever C3245 Pin 7, circuit CET34 (BN/GN) and ground.

Is the resistance greater than 10,000 ohms?

Yes GO to G6.
No REPAIR circuit CET34 (BN/GN) for a short to ground. CLEAR the DTC. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.


G6  CHECK THE TOW/HAUL INPUT CIRCUIT FOR AN OPEN
  • Connect the scan tool.
  • Enter the following diagnostic mode on the scan tool: TCS_DEPRES .
  • Connect a fused jumper from the selector lever switch, C3245 Pin 6 circuit CBP42 (GN) to C3245 Pin 7 circuit CET34 (BN/GN) harness side.

Does the TCS_DEPRES PID display YES?

Yes REPLACE the selector lever. REFER to Selector Lever — Floor Shift in this section. CLEAR the DTC. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.
No INSPECT and REPAIR circuit CET34 (BN/GN) for an open. If an open circuit is not found, REPLACE the PCM. PERFORM the Solenoid Body Strategy Data Download procedure. REFER to Section  307-01. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.



PINPOINT TEST H : THE SELECTSHIFT™ SWITCH IS NOT OPERATING CORRECTLY
NOTE: Vehicles equipped with a 3.7L engine use PCM C175B.
NOTE: Vehicles equipped with a 5.0L or 6.2L engine use PCM C1381B.
NOTE: Vehicles equipped with a 3.5L engine use PCM C1551B.
H1  CHECK DTCS
  • Ignition ON.
  • Connect the scan tool.
  • Start the engine and RUN the Key ON Engine Running (KOER) self-test.

Is DTC P0815 set?

Yes GO to H2.
No GO to H5.


H2  CHECK THE UPSHIFT CIRCUIT FOR VOLTAGE
  • Ignition OFF.
  • Disconnect: Selector Lever C3245.
  • Inspect C3245 for damaged, pushed-out terminals, corrosion or loose wires.
  • Ignition ON.
  • Measure the voltage between the selector lever C3245 Pin 9, circuit CET43 (GY), harness side and ground.

Is the voltage greater than 10 volts?

Yes GO to H4.
No GO to H3.


H3  CHECK THE UPSHIFT CIRCUIT FOR AN OPEN
  • Ignition OFF.
  • Disconnect: PCM C175B.
  • Disconnect: PCM C1381B.
  • Disconnect: PCM C1551B.
  • Inspect the connector for damaged, pushed-out terminals, corrosion or loose wires.
  • For 3.7L engine vehicles, measure the resistance between the Tow/Haul switch C3245 Pin 9, circuit CET43 (GY) and PCM C175B Pin 23, harness side.
  • For 5.0L or 6.2L engine vehicles, measure the resistance between the Tow/Haul switch C3245 Pin 9, circuit CET43 (GY) and PCM C1381B Pin 23, harness side.
  • For 3.5L engine vehicles, measure the resistance between the Tow/Haul switch C3245 Pin 9, circuit CET43 (GY) and PCM C1551B Pin 89, harness side.

Is the resistance less than 5 ohms?

Yes GO to H4.
No REPAIR circuit CET43 (GY) for an open. CLEAR the DTCs. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.


H4  CHECK THE SIGNAL RETURN CIRCUIT FOR AN OPEN
  • Connect: PCM C175B.
  • Connect: PCM C1381B.
  • Connect: PCM C1551B.
  • Ignition ON.
  • Measure the voltage between the selector lever C3245 Pin 9, circuit CET43 (GY) and C3245 Pin 11, circuit RE407 (YE/VT) harness side.

Is the voltage greater than 10 volts?

Yes INSTALL a new selector lever, REFER to Selector Lever — Floor Shift in this section. CLEAR the DTCs. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.
No REPAIR the signal return circuit RE407 (YE/VT) for an open. If an open circuit is not found, REPLACE the PCM. PROGRAM the PCM with the latest calibration level. PERFORM the solenoid strategy download procedure, REFER to solenoid body strategy in Section  307-01. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.


H5  CHECK THE DOWNSHIFT CIRCUIT FOR VOLTAGE
  • Ignition OFF.
  • Disconnect: Selector Lever C3245.
  • Inspect C3245 for damaged, pushed-out terminals, corrosion or loose wires.
  • Ignition ON.
  • Measure the voltage between the selector lever C3245 Pin 10, circuit CET42 (GN/VT), harness side and ground.

Is the voltage greater than 10 volts?

Yes GO to H7.
No GO to H6.


H6  CHECK THE DOWNSHIFT CIRCUIT FOR AN OPEN
  • Ignition OFF.
  • Disconnect: PCM C175B.
  • Disconnect: PCM C1381B.
  • Disconnect: PCM C1551B.
  • Inspect the connector for damaged, pushed-out terminals, corrosion or loose wires.
  • For 3.7L engine vehicles, measure the resistance between the selector lever C3245 Pin 10, circuit CET42 (GN/VT) and PCM C175B Pin 24, harness side.
  • For 5.0L or 6.2L engine vehicles, measure the resistance between the Tow/Haul switch C3245 Pin 10, circuit CET42 (GN/VT) and PCM C1381B Pin 24, harness side.
  • For 3.5L engine vehicles, measure the resistance between the Tow/Haul switch C3245 Pin 10, circuit CET42 (GN/VT) and PCM C1551B Pin 88, harness side.

Is the resistance less than 5 ohms?

Yes GO to H7.
No REPAIR circuit CET42 (GN/VT) for an open. CLEAR the DTCs. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.


H7  CHECK THE SIGNAL RETURN CIRCUIT FOR AN OPEN
  • Connect: PCM C175B.
  • Connect: PCM C1381B.
  • Connect: PCM C1551B.
  • Measure the voltage between the selector lever C3245 Pin 10, circuit CET42 (GN/VT) and C3245 Pin 11, circuit RE407 (YE/VT) harness side.

Is the voltage greater than 10 volts?

Yes INSTALL a new selector lever, REFER to Selector Lever — Floor Shift in this section. CLEAR the DTCs. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.
No INSPECT and REPAIR the signal return circuit RE407 (YE/VT) for an open. If an open circuit is not found, REPLACE the PCM. PROGRAM the PCM with the latest calibration level. PERFORM the solenoid strategy download procedure, REFER to solenoid body strategy in Section  307-01. RERUN the KOER self-test and test the system for normal operation.
