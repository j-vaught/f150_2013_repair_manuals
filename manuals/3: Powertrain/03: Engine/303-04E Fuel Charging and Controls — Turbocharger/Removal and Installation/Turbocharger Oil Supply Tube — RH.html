303-04E Fuel Charging and Controls — Turbocharger 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 06/28/2019

Turbocharger Oil Supply Tube — RH


Material
Item Specification
Motorcraft® SAE 5W-30 Synthetic Blend Motor Oil (US); Motorcraft® SAE 5W-30 Super Premium Motor Oil (Canada)
XO-5W30-QSP (US); CXO-5W30-LSP12 (Canada) 		WSS-M2C946-A

Removal

  1. Remove the Turbocharger - RH, refer to Turbocharger — RH.
  1. Remove the Starter Motor, refer to Section  303-06.
  1. Remove the lower turbocharger oil tube assembly bolt and the turbocharger oil tube assembly.
  1. Remove and discard the turbocharger oil tube assembly O-ring seal.
  1. Remove and discard the turbocharger fitting and oil filter assembly.

Installation

  1. Lubricate the turbocharger fitting and oil filter assembly O-ring with clean engine oil.
  1. Install the turbocharger fitting and oil filter assembly.
    • Tighten to 32 Nm (24 lb-ft).
  1. Install the new turbocharger oil tube assembly O-ring seal. Lubricate the new turbocharger oil tube assembly O-ring seal with clean engine oil.

  1. NOTE: Lubricate the cylinder block bore with clean engine oil.

    Install the turbocharger oil tube assembly and install the turbocharger oil tube assembly lower bolt.
    • Tighten the turbocharger oil tube assembly lower bolt in the following 2 stages.
      • Tighten to 8 Nm (71 lb-in).
      • Tighten an additional 30 degrees.
  1. Install the Starter Motor, refer to Section  303-06.
  1. Install the Turbocharger - RH, refer to Turbocharger — RH.
