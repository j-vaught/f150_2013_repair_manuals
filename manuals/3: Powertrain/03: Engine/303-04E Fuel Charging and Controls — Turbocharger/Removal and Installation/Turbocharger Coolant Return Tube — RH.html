303-04E Fuel Charging and Controls — Turbocharger 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 04/24/2015

Turbocharger Coolant Return Tube — RH

Removal

  1. Drain the cooling system, refer to Section  303-03.
  1. Remove the brake vacuum pump, refer to Section  206-07.

  1. NOTE: Use a 3/8" Jiffy-Tite® quick disconnect tool, close the tool around the tube, press the tool into the fitting and rotate until the tool is fully seated into the fitting.

    Disconnect and remove the turbocharger coolant return tube.

Installation

  1. Inspect the turbocharger coolant return tube fittings and replace as needed. Tighten to 18 Nm (159 lb-in).
    1. Install the turbocharger coolant return tube and connect the turbocharger coolant return tube at the engine.
    1. Connect the turbocharger coolant return tube at the turbocharger.
  1. Install the brake vacuum pump, refer to Section  206-07.
  1. Fill and bleed the cooling system, refer to Section  303-03.
