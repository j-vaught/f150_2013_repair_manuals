303-04E Fuel Charging and Controls — Turbocharger 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 06/28/2019

Turbocharger Oil Return Tube — RH

Removal

  1. The turbocharger oil return tube is serviced with the turbocharger oil supply tube.
    Remove the Turbocharger Oil Supply Tube - RH, refer to Turbocharger Oil Supply Tube — RH.

Installation

  1. Install the Turbocharger Oil Supply Tube - RH, refer to Turbocharger Oil Supply Tube — RH.
