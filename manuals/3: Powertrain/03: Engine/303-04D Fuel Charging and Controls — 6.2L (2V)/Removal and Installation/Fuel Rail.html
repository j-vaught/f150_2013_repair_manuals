Removal

WARNING: Do not smoke, carry lighted tobacco or have an open flame of any type when working on or near any fuel-related component. Highly flammable mixtures are always present and may be ignited. Failure to follow these instructions may result in serious personal injury.

WARNING: Before working on or disconnecting any of the fuel tubes or fuel system components, relieve the fuel system pressure to prevent accidental spraying of fuel. Fuel in the fuel system remains under high pressure, even when the engine is not running. Failure to follow this instruction may result in serious personal injury.

NOTICE: Fuel injection equipment is manufactured to very precise tolerances and fine clearances. It is therefore essential that absolute cleanliness is observed when working with these components. Always install blanking plugs to any open orifices or tubes.

NOTICE: When reusing liquid or vapor tube connectors, make sure to use compressed air to remove any foreign material from the connector retaining clip area before separating from the tube.

  1. Disconnect the battery ground cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Remove the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet tube. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Disconnect the heater coolant hose retainer from the ACL outlet pipe-to-Throttle Body (TB) adapter.
  1. Disconnect the crankcase ventilation tube quick connect fitting from the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter. For additional information, refer to Section  310-00.
  1. Disconnect the brake booster vacuum hose from the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter.
  1. Loosen the clamp and disconnect the TB adapter from the TB .
  1. Remove the 2 bolts and the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter.
  1. Disconnect the fuel tube quick connect coupling. For additional information, refer to Section  310-00.
  1. Disconnect the 8 ignition coil electrical connectors.
  1. Disconnect the 8 ignition wires from the 8 ignition coils.
  1. Disconnect the 8 fuel injector electrical connectors.
  1. Remove the 4 fuel rail bolts and the fuel rail and injectors as an assembly.
  1. Remove the fuel injector-to-fuel rail locks and separate the 8 fuel injectors from the fuel rail.
    • Discard the upper and lower fuel injector O-ring seals.

Installation

  1. NOTICE: Use O-ring seals that are made of special fuel-resistant material. Use of ordinary O-rings can cause the fuel system to leak.

    NOTE: Lubricate the new O-ring seals with clean engine oil prior to installation.

    Install 16 new O-ring seals on each of the fuel injectors.
  1. Assemble the 8 fuel injectors onto the fuel rail and install the 8 fuel injector retaining clips.
  1. Install the fuel rail and fuel injectors as an assembly onto the intake manifold.
  1. Install the 4 fuel rail bolts in 2 stages.
    • Stage 1: Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
    • Stage 2: Tighten an additional 90 degrees.
  1. Connect the 8 fuel injector electrical connectors.
  1. Connect the 8 ignition wires to the 8 ignition coils.
  1. Connect the 8 ignition coil electrical connectors.
  1. Connect the fuel tube quick connect coupling. For additional information, refer to Section  310-00.
  1. Position the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter and install the 2 bolts.
    • Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
  1. Tighten the TB adapter-to-TB clamp.
    • Tighten to 5 Nm (44 lb-in).
  1. Connect the brake booster vacuum hose from the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter.
  1. Connect the crankcase ventilation tube quick connect fitting to theACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter. For additional information, refer to Section  310-00.
  1. Connect the heater coolant hose retainer to the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter.
  1. Install the ACL outlet tube. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Connect the battery ground cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
