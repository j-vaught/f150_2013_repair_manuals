Removal

  1. Remove the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet tube. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Disconnect the heater coolant hose retainer from the ACL outlet pipe-to-Throttle Body (TB) adapter.
  1. Disconnect the crankcase ventilation tube quick connect fitting from ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter. For additional information, refer to Section  310-00.
  1. Disconnect the brake booster vacuum hose from the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter.
  1. Remove the 2 bolts and the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter.
  1. Loosen the clamp and disconnect the TB adapter from the TB .
  1. Disconnect the Electronic Throttle Control (ETC) electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the TB assembly.
    • Discard the TB O-ring seal.

Installation

  1. Using a new O-ring seal, position the TB and tighten the 4 bolts in 2 stages.
    • Stage 1: Tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
    • Stage 2: Tighten an additional 60 degrees.
  1. Connect the ETC electrical connector.
  1. Position the TB adapter onto the TB and install the 2 bolts.
    • Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
  1. Tighten the TB adapter-to-TB clamp.
    • Tighten to 5 Nm (44 lb-in).
  1. Connect the crankcase ventilation tube quick connect coupling to the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter. For additional information, refer to Section  310-00.
  1. Connect the brake booster vacuum hose to the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter.
  1. Connect the crankcase ventilation tube quick connect fitting to the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter. For additional information, refer to Section  310-00.
  1. Connect the heater coolant hose retainer to the ACL outlet pipe-to-TB adapter.
  1. Install the ACL outlet tube. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
