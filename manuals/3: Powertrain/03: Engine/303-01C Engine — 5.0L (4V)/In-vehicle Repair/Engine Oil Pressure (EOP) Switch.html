303-01C Engine — 5.0L (4V) 2013 F-150
In-vehicle Repair Procedure revision date: 07/20/2015

Engine Oil Pressure (EOP) Switch


Material
Item Specification
Motorcraft® Thread Sealant with PTFE
TA-24-B 		WSK-M2G350-A2

Removal

4x2 vehicles

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. Refer to Section  100-02.

4x4 vehicles

  1. Remove the generator. Refer to Section  414-00.

All vehicles

  1. Disconnect the EOP switch electrical connector.
    • Remove the EOP switch.

Installation

All vehicles

  1. NOTE: If the oil pressure switch is to be reused, apply thread sealant.

    Install the EOP switch.
    • Tighten in 2 stages.
      • Stage 1: Tighten to 14 Nm (124 lb-in).
      • Stage 2: Tighten an additional 180 degrees.
    • Connect the EOP switch electrical connector.

4x4 vehicles

  1. Install the generator. Refer to Section  414-00.
