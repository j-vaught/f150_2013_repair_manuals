303-01C Engine — 5.0L (4V) 2013 F-150
In-vehicle Repair Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Flexplate


Special Tool(s)
Vehicle Communication Module (VCM) and Integrated Diagnostic System (IDS) software with appropriate hardware, or equivalent scan tool

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the transmission. For additional information, refer to Section  307-01.

  1. NOTE: The flexplate bolts are torque-to-yield design and are not reusable.

    Remove the 8 bolts and the flexplate.
    • Discard the bolts.
    • To install, tighten in the sequence shown in 2 stages.
      • Stage 1: Tighten to 20 Nm (177 lb-in).
      • Stage 2: Tighten an additional 60 degrees.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • After completing the repairs, use the scan tool to perform the Misfire Monitor Neutral Profile Correction procedure, following the on-screen instructions.
