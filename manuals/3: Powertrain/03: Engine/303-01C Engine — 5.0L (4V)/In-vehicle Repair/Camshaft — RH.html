Removal

NOTICE: During engine repair procedures, cleanliness is extremely important. Any foreign material, including any material created while cleaning gasket surfaces, that enters the oil passages, coolant passages or the oil pan, can cause engine failure.

NOTE: If the components are to be reinstalled, they must be installed in their original location. Mark the components for installation into their original location.

  1. Remove the engine front cover. For additional information, refer to Engine Front Cover in this section.
  1. Using the crankshaft holding tool, rotate the crankshaft clockwise until the crankshaft keyway is at the 12 o'clock position.
  1. Verify the data matrix on the camshafts is facing up, if not, rotate the crankshaft clockwise one revolution.
  1. Remove the 2 bolts and the RH primary timing chain tensioner.

  1. NOTE: It may be necessary to rotate the crankshaft slightly to provide enough slack in the chain to remove the RH timing chain tensioner arm. Return the crankshaft keyway to the 12 o'clock position after removing the RH timing chain tensioner arm.

    Remove the RH timing chain tensioner arm.

  1. NOTE: It may be necessary to rotate the crankshaft slightly to provide enough slack in the chain to remove the RH timing chain guide. Return the crankshaft keyway to the 12 o'clock position after removing the RH timing chain guide.

    Remove the bolt and the RH timing chain guide.
  1. Remove the RH primary timing chain.
  1. Remove and discard the 3 RH intake Variable Camshaft Timing (VCT) assembly bolts and the 3 RH exhaust VCT assembly bolts.
  1. Slide the RH VCT assemblies and secondary timing chain forward 2 mm (0.078 in).
  1. Depress the RH secondary timing chain tensioner and turn the tensioner 90 degrees.
  1. Remove the RH VCT assemblies and the RH secondary timing chain.

  1. NOTE: Intake camshaft shown, exhaust camshaft similar.

    Remove the VCT system oil filter from the intake and exhaust camshafts.

  1. NOTICE: The front camshaft bearing mega cap must be removed first and then the remaining camshaft bearing caps. Failure to follow this direction may result in damage to the engine.

    Remove the 4 bolts and the RH front camshaft bearing mega cap.
  1. Remove the 16 bolts and the 8 camshaft bearing caps.
  1. Remove the RH intake and exhaust camshafts.

Installation

  1. NOTE: Lubricate the camshafts with clean engine oil prior to installation.

    Install the RH intake and exhaust camshafts in the neutral position. Align the D-slots as shown in the illustration.
  1. Install the 8 camshaft bearing caps and the 16 bolts. Do not tighten the bolts at this time.
  1. Install the RH front camshaft bearing mega cap and the 4 bolts. Do not tighten at this time
  1. Tighten the bolts in the sequence shown in 2 stages.
    • Stage 1: Tighten to 6 Nm (53 lb-in).
    • Stage 2: Tighten an additional 45 degrees.

  1. NOTE: Intake camshaft shown, exhaust camshaft similar.

    Install the VCT system oil filter in the intake and exhaust camshafts.
  1. Install the secondary timing chain onto the RH VCT assemblies. Align the colored links on the secondary timing chain with the timing marks on the VCT assemblies as shown in the illustration.
    1. The timing mark on the intake VCT assembly should align between the 2 consecutive colored links.
    1. The timing mark on the exhaust VCT assembly should align with the single colored link.
  1. Install the RH VCT assemblies and the secondary timing chain onto the RH camshafts to a position 2 mm (0.078 in) from fully seated. The timing mark on the exhaust VCT assembly should be in the 1 o'clock position.

  1. NOTE: It may be necessary to rotate the exhaust camshaft slightly (using a wrench on the flats of the camshaft) to seat the VCT assemblies onto the camshafts.

    Rotate the secondary timing chain tensioner 90 degrees so the ramped area is facing forward and fully seat the VCT assemblies onto the camshafts.
    • If the secondary timing chain is not centered over the tensioner, reposition the VCT assemblies until they are fully seated on the camshafts.

  1. NOTE: Use a wrench on the flats of the camshaft to hold the camshafts while tightening the VCT assembly bolts.

    Install the 3 new RH intake VCT assembly bolts and the 3 new RH exhaust VCT assembly bolts.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in) plus an additional 90 degrees.
  1. Install the RH primary timing chain.
    • Align the colored link on the timing chain with the timing mark on the RH VCT assembly.
  1. Align the remaining colored link on the timing chain with the timing mark on the crankshaft sprocket.

  1. NOTE: It may be necessary to rotate the crankshaft slightly to provide enough slack in the chain to install the RH timing chain guide. Return the crankshaft keyway to the 12 o'clock position after installing the RH timing chain guide.

    Install the RH timing chain guide and bolt.
    • Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).

  1. NOTE: It may be necessary to rotate the crankshaft slightly to provide enough slack in the chain to install the RH timing chain tensioner arm. Return the crankshaft keyway to the 12 o'clock position after installing the RH timing chain tensioner arm.

    Install the RH timing chain tensioner arm.

  1. NOTICE: Do not compress the ratchet assembly or damage to the tensioner will occur.

    Compress the primary timing chain tensioner plunger, using an edge of a vise.
  1. Using a small screwdriver or pick, push back and hold the ratchet mechanism, then push the ratchet arm back into the tensioner housing.
  1. Install a suitable pin into the hole of the tensioner housing to hold the ratchet assembly and plunger in place during installation.
  1. Install the RH primary timing chain tensioner and 2 bolts.
    • Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
    • Remove the holding pin from the tensioner.
  1. Install the engine front cover. For additional information, refer to Engine Front Cover in this section.
