303-04C Fuel Charging and Controls — 5.0L (4V) 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Throttle Body


Item Part Number Description
1 9E9369E936 Throttle Body (TB) gasket
2 9E9269E926 TB
3 — Electronic throttle control electrical connector (part of 12A581)
4 W714866W714866 TB bolt (4 required)

Removal

  1. Remove the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet pipe. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Disconnect the Electronic Throttle Control (ETC) electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the Throttle Body (TB)
    • Discard the gasket.

Installation

  1. Using a new gasket, position the TB and install the 4 bolts loosely.
  1. While holding the TB in the most upward position tighten the 4 bolts in 2 stages.
    • Stage 1: Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
    • Stage 2: Tighten an additional 45 degrees.
  1. Connect the ETC electrical connector.
  1. Install the ACL outlet pipe. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
