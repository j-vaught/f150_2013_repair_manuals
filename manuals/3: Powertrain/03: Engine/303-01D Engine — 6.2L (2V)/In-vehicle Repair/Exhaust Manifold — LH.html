Removal

All vehicles

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information, refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Remove the intake manifold. For additional information, refer to Intake Manifold in this section.
  1. Remove the LH inner fenderwell. For additional information, refer to Section  501-02.
  1. Install the Engine Support Bar and the Engine Support Bracket.

  1. NOTICE: Do not allow the steering column shaft to rotate while the intermediate shaft is disconnected or damage to the clockspring can result. If there is evidence that the shaft has rotated, the clockspring must be removed and recentered. For additional information, refer to Section  501-20B.

    Remove the bolt and disconnect the steering shaft and position aside.
  1. Remove the 4 exhaust manifold-to-catalytic converter nuts (2 LH and 2 RH).

  1. NOTICE: Only use hand tools when removing the transmission mount-to-crossmember nuts or damage to the transmission mount can occur.

    Loosen the 2 transmission mount-to-crossmember nuts.

  1. NOTICE: Only use hand tools when removing the engine support insulator nuts or the engine support insulator may be damaged.

    NOTE: If during nut removal the stud bolt is extracted from the engine support insulator, separate the nut from the stud bolt prior to stud bolt installation.

    Remove the 2 RH engine support insulator nuts.

Four-Wheel Drive (4WD) vehicles

  1. Remove the front driveshaft. For additional information, refer to Section  205-01.

All vehicles

  1. NOTICE: Only use hand tools when removing the engine support insulator through bolt or the engine support insulator may be damaged.

    Remove the LH engine support insulator bolt.
  1. Using the Engine Support Bar and Engine Support Bracket, raise the engine.
  1. Remove the 2 bolts and the exhaust shield.
  1. Remove the 8 exhaust manifold nuts, the 8 studs, the exhaust manifold and the 2 exhaust manifold gaskets.
    • Discard the exhaust manifold nuts, studs and gaskets.

  1. NOTICE: Do not use metal scrapers, wire brushes, power abrasive discs or other abrasive means to clean the sealing surfaces. These may cause scratches and gouges resulting in leak paths. Use a plastic scraper to clean the sealing surfaces.

    NOTE: Clean the sealing surfaces with metal surface prep. Follow the directions on the packaging.

    Remove and discard the 2 exhaust manifold gaskets. Clean the sealing surfaces with metal surface prep.
  1. Inspect the exhaust manifold. For additional information, refer to Section  303-00.

Installation

All vehicles

  1. Using 2 new exhaust manifold gaskets and 8 new studs, position the 2 gaskets and exhaust manifold and install the 8 studs.
    • Tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. Using new exhaust manifold nuts, install the 8 nuts in 2 stages in the sequence shown.
    • Stage 1: Tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
    • Stage 2: Tighten to 32 Nm (24 lb-ft).
  1. Position the exhaust manifold heat shield and install the 2 bolts.
    • Tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
  1. Using the Engine Support Bar and Engine Support Bracket, lower the engine.

  1. NOTICE: Only use hand tools when tightening the engine support insulator through bolt or the engine support insulator may be damaged.

    Apply threadlock to the bolt threads and install the LH engine support insulator bolt.
    • Tighten to 350 Nm (258 lb-ft).

Four-Wheel Drive (4WD) vehicles

  1. Install the front driveshaft. For additional information, refer to Section  205-01.

All vehicles

  1. NOTICE: Only use hand tools when tightening the engine support insulator nuts or the engine support insulator may be damaged.

    NOTE: Make sure the RH engine support insulator mating surfaces and the washer mating surface are free of foreign material and corrosion before installation. Install a new washer.

    Apply threadlock to the stud threads and install the RH engine support insulator washer and nuts.
    • Tighten to 175 Nm (129 lb-ft).
  1. Tighten the 2 transmission mount nuts.
    • Tighten to 103 Nm (76 lb-ft).
  1. Install the 4 exhaust Y-pipe flange nuts (2 RH and 2 LH).
    • Tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).

  1. NOTICE: Do not allow the steering column shaft to rotate while the intermediate shaft is disconnected or damage to the clockspring can result. If there is evidence that the shaft has rotated, the clockspring must be removed and recentered. For additional information, refer to Section  501-20B.

    Connect the steering shaft and install the bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 30 Nm (22 lb-ft).
  1. Install the LH inner fenderwell. For additional information, refer to Section  501-02.
  1. Remove the Engine Support Bar and Engine Support Bracket.
  1. Install the intake manifold. For additional information, refer to Intake Manifold in this section.
