Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect the PCV tube quick connect coupling from the PCV. For additional information, refer to Section  310-00.

  1. NOTICE: A new PCV valve must be installed if removed. During removal, the plastic retaining ears of the PCV valve are sheared.

    Rotate the PCV valve counterclockwise and remove the PCV valve.
    • Discard the PCV valve.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Install a new PCV valve.
