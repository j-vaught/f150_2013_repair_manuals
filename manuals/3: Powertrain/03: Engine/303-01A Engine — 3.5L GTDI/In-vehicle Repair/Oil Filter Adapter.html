303-01A Engine — 3.5L GTDI 2013 F-150
In-vehicle Repair Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Oil Filter Adapter

Removal

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Disconnect the battery ground cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Remove the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet pipe and the LH ACL -to-turbocharger pipe. For additional information, refer to Intake Air System Components — Exploded View, 3.5L Gasoline Turbocharged Direct Injection (GTDI) in Section  303-12.
  1. If equipped, rotate the 4 retainers and position the air deflector down. Rotate the skid plate cover counterclockwise and remove.
  1. Remove and discard the engine oil filter.

  1. NOTICE: If metal or foreign material is present in the oil cooler, mechanical concerns exist. To diagnose the mechanical concerns, refer to Section  303-00.

    Remove the oil cooler insert bolt and position the oil cooler aside.
    • Inspect the oil cooler gasket, replace if necessary.
  1. Disconnect the generator electrical connector.
  1. Position the generator B+ protective boot aside, remove the B+ nut and position the B+ terminal aside.
    • Detach the generator wiring harness retainer from the oil filter adapter and position the generator wiring harness aside.
  1. Disconnect the Engine Oil Pressure (EOP) electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 5 bolts and the oil filter adapter.
    • Discard the gasket.
    • Clean and inspect all sealing surfaces.

Installation

  1. Using a new gasket, install the oil filter adapter and the 5 bolts. Tighten in sequence shown.
    • Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in) and then an additional 45 degrees.
  1. Connect the EOP electrical connector.
  1. Position back the generator wiring harness and B+ terminal and install the nut.
    • Tighten to 17 Nm (150 lb-in).
    • Position back the generator B+ protective boot.
    • Attach the generator wiring harness retainer to the oil filter adapter.
  1. Connect the generator electrical connector.
  1. Position back the oil cooler and install the oil cooler insert bolt.
    • Tighten to 58 Nm (43 lb-ft).

  1. NOTE: Do not lubricate the engine oil filter gasket.

    Install the engine oil filter.
    • Tighten to 5 Nm (44 lb-in) and then rotate an additional 180 degrees.
  1. If equipped, position the air deflector and fasten the 4 retainers. Install the skid plate cover.
  1. Install the ACL outlet pipe and the LH ACL -to-turbocharger pipe. For additional information, refer to Intake Air System Components — Exploded View, 3.5L GTDI in Section  303-12.
  1. Connect the battery ground cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Inspect and adjust the engine oil level.
