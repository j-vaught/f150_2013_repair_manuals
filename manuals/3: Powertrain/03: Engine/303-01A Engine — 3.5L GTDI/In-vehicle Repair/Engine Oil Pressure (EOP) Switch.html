303-01A Engine — 3.5L GTDI 2013 F-150
In-vehicle Repair Procedure revision date: 09/23/2015

Engine Oil Pressure (EOP) Switch


Material
Item Specification
Motorcraft® Thread Sealant with PTFE
TA-24-B 		WSK-M2G350-A2

Removal

4x2

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. Refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Remove the retainers and the underbody shield.

4x4

  1. Remove the engine appearance cover.
  1. Remove the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet pipe. Refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Remove the LH ACL pipe-to-turbocharger pipe and the LH turbocharger-to-Charge Air Cooler (CAC) pipe. Refer to Section  303-12.

4x2/4x4

  1. Disconnect the Engine Oil Pressure (EOP) switch electrical connector.
  1. Remove the EOP switch.

Installation

4x2/4x4

  1. NOTE: If the oil pressure switch is to be reused, apply thread sealant.

    Install the EOP switch.
    • Tighten to 14 Nm (124 lb-in) then an additional 180 degrees.
  1. Connect the EOP switch electrical connector.

4x4

  1. Install the LH ACL pipe-to-turbocharger pipe and the LH turbocharger-to-CAC pipe. Refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Install the ACL outlet pipe. Refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Install the engine appearance cover.

4x2

  1. Install the underbody shield and the retainers.
