3.7L Air Cleaner (ACL) Assembly

NOTICE: Fully seat the Air Cleaner (ACL) element into the ACL housing. Failure to do so will result in unusual engine noise.

NOTE: Disconnect the crankcase vent hose quick connect coupling from the air cleaner outlet pipe as necessary. Refer to Section  310-00.

NOTE: Securely seal the ACL outlet pipe to prevent unmetered air from entering the engine.

NOTE: The ACL housing is part of the degas bottle assembly. Refer to Section  303-03.
