Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the thermostat housing. For additional information, refer to Thermostat Housing — 3.5L GTDI in this section.
  1. Remove the cooling fan motor and shroud. For additional information, refer to Cooling Fan Motor and Shroud.
  1. Remove the coolant inlet pipe.
    • Remove and discard the O-ring seal.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Install a new O-ring seal and lubricate it with clean engine coolant.
