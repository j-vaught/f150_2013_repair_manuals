Removal

All engines

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information, refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Drain the cooling system. For additional information, refer to Cooling System Draining, Filling and Bleeding in this section.
  1. Evacuate the A/C system. For additional information, refer to Section  412-00.
  1. Disconnect the battery ground. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Remove the headlights. For additional information, refer to Section  417-01.
  1. Detach the LH hood latch cable retainer.
  1. Detach the 2 RH hood latch cable retainers.
  1. Remove the bolts and position the hood latch aside.
  1. Remove the bolt, detach the hood latch release handle and the cable from the front bumper cover, and position the hood latch release handle aside.

  1. NOTE: LH shown, RH similar.

    If equipped, remove the 4 LH and 4 RH front wheel moulding pushpins.

  1. NOTE: LH shown, RH similar.

    If equipped, remove the 2 LH and 2 RH front wheel moulding nuts and detach the front wheel mouldings far enough to remove the front bumper cover.

  1. NOTE: LH shown, RH similar.

    Remove the 3 LH and 3 RH bumper cover-to-fender bolts.
  1. Remove the 6 bumper cover bracket bolts and remove the bumper cover and the brackets as an assembly.
  1. Disconnect the horn electrical connector and detach the wiring harness and ambient temperature sensor retainers. Position the wiring harness aside.
  1. Detach the wiring harness retainer from the RH side of the radiator grille support.

  1. NOTE: RH shown, LH similar.

    Disconnect the RH and LH front impact severity sensor electrical connectors and 2 wiring harness retainers. Position the wiring harnesses aside.
  1. Release the clamp and disconnect the upper radiator hose from the radiator.
  1. Remove the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet tube. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Disconnect the Mass Air Flow (MAF) sensor electrical connector.

6.2L engines

  1. Detach the upper radiator hose retainer from the cooling fan motor and shroud.
  1. Remove the nut, detach the generator battery positive cable, detach the 3 wiring harness retainers from the cooling fan shroud and position the wiring harness aside.

3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Remove the 4 pushpins and the upper radiator air deflector.
  1. Detach the wiring harness retainer from the stud bolt on the LH side of the cooling fan shroud.
  1. Disconnect the positive battery cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Detach the battery cable retainer from the cooling fan shroud.

6.2L engines

  1. Detach the power steering fluid cooler hose retainer and position the hose aside.
  1. Syphon the power steering fluid from the power steering reservoir, release the clamp and disconnect the power steering fluid cooler return hose on top of the radiator.
  1. Detach the wiring harness retainer from the power steering fluid reservoir stud bolt.
  1. Remove the power steering fluid reservoir stud bolt and position aside the reservoir.

All engines

  1. If equipped, cut the block heater tie strap.
  1. Remove the bolt and nut, and disconnect the A/C tube fittings from the A/C condenser.
    • Discard the O-ring seals and gaskets.
  1. Disconnect the 2 cooling fan electrical connectors and detach the 3 cooling fan wiring harness retainers.
  1. Release the 4 Battery Junction Box (BJB) retaining tabs and detach the BJB from the bracket.

  1. NOTE: Push the 2 LH wiring harness retainer tabs outward to release.

    Detach the LH wiring harness retainer and the 3 RH wiring harness pushpins and position the BJB , the wiring harness.

3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Release the clamp and disconnect the radiator-to-degas bottle hose from the radiator.

  1. NOTE: The quick connect coupling illustrated is to show the spring clip release location only. The quick connect coupling on the hose may differ.

    Pull the lower radiator hose spring clip up until the end of the clip is in the detent on the quick connect coupling and disconnect the lower radiator hose from the radiator.

6.2L engines

  1. Remove the spring clip and disconnect the lower radiator hose from the radiator.
  1. Disconnect the radiator overflow hose from the radiator.
  1. Remove the 3 front air deflector-to-bumper pushpins.

All engines

  1. Remove the 2 RH air deflector-to-frame rail pushpins.

3.5L GTDI engines

  1. Turn the 4 quarter-turn retainers, detach the 8 pushpins and remove the lower air deflector.

3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Remove the 2 air dam bolts and the air dam.
  1. Remove the 3 front air deflector-to-bumper pushpins.

All engines

  1. Remove the 2 transmission cooler tube latch assemblies.

  1. NOTE: 6.2L shown, 3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L similar.

    Using the Transmission Cooler Line Disconnect Tool, disconnect the transmission fluid cooler tubes.

3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. For vehicles without an auxiliary transmission fluid cooler, remove the transmission cooler tube bracket bolt and detach the transmission cooler tube retainer from the crossmember.
  1. For vehicles with an auxiliary transmission fluid cooler, remove the transmission cooler tube bracket bolt and detach the 2 transmission cooler tube retainers from the crossmember.

6.2L engines

  1. Disconnect the power steering cooler-to-power steering fluid reservoir hose from the power steering fluid reservoir.

All engines

  1. Remove the 6 cooling module bolts.
  1. Remove the cooling module.

Installation

All engines

  1. Position the cooling module into the vehicle.
  1. Install the 6 cooling module bolts.
    • Tighten to 30 Nm (22 lb-ft).

6.2L engines

  1. Connect the power steering cooler-to-power steering fluid reservoir hose at the power steering reservoir.

3.5L Gasoline Turbocharged Direct Injection (GTDI) , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. For vehicles with an auxiliary transmission fluid cooler, attach the 2 transmission cooler tube retainers and install the transmission cooler tube bracket bolt.
    • Tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
  1. For vehicles without an auxiliary transmission fluid cooler, attach the transmission cooler tube retainer and install the transmission cooler tube bracket bolt.
    • Tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).

All vehicles

  1. Connect the transmission cooler tubes.
  1. Install the transmission cooler tube latch assemblies.

3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Install the 3 air deflector-to-front bumper pushpins.
  1. Position the air dam and install the 2 bolts.
    • Tighten to 20 Nm (177 lb-in).

3.5L GTDI engines

  1. Position the lower air deflector, attach the 8 pushpins and turn the 4 quarter-turn retainers.

6.2L engines

  1. Install the 3 front air deflector-to-bumper pushpins.

All engines

  1. Install the 2 RH air deflector-to-frame rail pushpins.

3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Push the spring clip in place and connect the lower radiator hose quick connect coupling to the radiator, making sure the coupling is secure.
  1. Connect the radiator-to-degas bottle hose to the radiator and position the clamp.

6.2L engines

  1. Connect the radiator overflow hose to the radiator.
  1. Connect the lower radiator hose to the radiator and install the spring clip.

All engines

  1. Position the BJB and wiring harness into place, connect the BJB retaining tabs, and attach the LH wiring harness retainer and the 3 RH wiring harness pushpins.
  1. Attach the 3 cooling fan wiring harness retainers and connect the 2 cooling fan electrical connectors.
  1. Install new O-ring seals and gaskets on the A/C condenser fittings.
  1. Connect the A/C fittings to the A/C condenser and install the bolt and the nut.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. If equipped, install a new block heater tie strap.

3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Attach the wiring harness retainer to the stud bolt on the LH side of the cooling fan shroud.
  1. Connect the battery positive cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Attach the battery cable retainer to the cooling fan shroud.
  1. Install the upper radiator air deflector and install the 4 pushpins.

6.2L engines

  1. Position the power steering fluid reservoir and install the stud bolt.
    • Tighten to 11 Nm (97 lb-in).
  1. Attach the wiring harness retainer to the power steering fluid reservoir stud bolt.
  1. Connect the power steering fluid cooler return hose on top of the radiator.
  1. Connect the power steering fluid cooler hose retainer.
  1. Position the wiring harness, attach the 3 wiring harness retainers to the cooling fan shroud, attach the generator battery positive cable and install the nut.
    • Tighten to 9 Nm (80 lb-in).
  1. Attach the upper radiator hose retainer to the cooling fan motor and shroud.

All engines

  1. Connect the MAF sensor electrical connector.
  1. Install the ACL outlet tube. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Connect the upper radiator hose to the radiator and install the clamp.

  1. NOTE: RH shown, LH similar.

    Position the wiring harness into place and connect the RH and LH front impact severity sensor electrical connectors and wiring harness pushpin retainers.
  1. Attach the wiring harness retainer to the RH side of the radiator grille support.
  1. Position the wiring harness into place and connect the horn and ambient temperature sensor electrical connectors and attach the pushpin retainer.
  1. Position the front bumper cover and the brackets as an assembly and install the 6 bumper cover bracket bolts.
    • Tighten to 3 Nm (27 lb-in).

  1. NOTE: LH shown, RH similar.

    Install the 3 LH and 3 RH bumper cover-to-fender bolts.
    • Tighten to 3 Nm (27 lb-in).

  1. NOTE: LH shown, RH similar.

    If equipped, attach the front wheel mouldings and install the 2 LH and 2 RH nuts.
    • Tighten to 3 Nm (27 lb-in).

  1. NOTE: LH shown, RH similar.

    If equipped, install the 4 LH and 4 RH front wheel moulding pushpins.
  1. Position and attach the hood latch release handle and cable onto the front bumper cover and install the bolt.
    • Tighten to 8 Nm (71 lb-in).
  1. Position the hood latch into place and install the 2 bolts.
    • Tighten to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
  1. Attach the 2 RH hood latch cable pushpin retainers.
  1. Attach the LH hood latch cable pushpin retainers.
  1. Install the headlights. For additional information, refer to Section  417-01.
  1. Connect the battery ground cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Fill the cooling system. For additional information, refer to Cooling System Draining, Filling and Bleeding in this section.
  1. Recharge the A/C system. For additional information, refer to Section  412-00.
  1. If equipped, fill the power steering system. For additional information, refer to Section  211-00B.
  1. Check the transmission fluid and fill as necessary. For additional information, refer to Section  307-01.
