Removal

All engines

  1. Recover the A/C system. For additional information, refer to Section  412-00.
  1. Drain the cooling system.
  1. Remove the cooling fan motor and shroud. For additional information, refer to Cooling Fan Motor and Shroud in this section.

3.5L Gasoline Turbocharged Direct Injection (GTDI) , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Release the clamp and disconnect the upper radiator hose from the radiator.
  1. Release the clamp and disconnect the radiator-to-degas bottle hose from the radiator.

  1. NOTE: The quick connect coupling illustrated is to show the spring clip release location only. The quick connect coupling on the hose may differ.

    Pull the lower radiator hose spring clip up until the end of the clip is in the detent on the quick connect coupling and disconnect the lower radiator hose from the radiator.
  1. Disconnect the Mass Air Flow (MAF) sensor electrical connector.

  1. NOTE: 6.2L shown, 3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L similar.

    Remove the 2 bolts and position the coolant expansion tank (6.2L) or the degas bottle (3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L) aside.

6.2L engines

  1. Release the clamp and disconnect the upper radiator hose from the radiator.
  1. Disconnect the overflow hose from the radiator.
  1. Remove the spring clip and disconnect the lower radiator hose from the radiator.
  1. Release the clamp and disconnect the power steering cooler hose from the power steering cooler.

All engines

  1. Disconnect the 3 RH and 2 LH air deflector-to-condenser pushpin retainers.

3.5L GTDI engines

  1. Turn the 4 quarter-turn retainers, detach the 8 pushpins and remove the lower air deflector.

3.7L, 5.0L and 6.2L engines

  1. Remove the 4 lower radiator air deflector-to-A/C condenser pushpins.

All engines

  1. Remove the bolt and the nut and disconnect the A/C condenser fittings.
    • Discard the gasket seals and the O-ring seals.
  1. Remove the transmission cooler tube secondary latches.
  1. Using the Transmission Cooler Line Disconnect Tool, disconnect the transmission fluid cooler tubes.
  1. Detach the wiring harness retainer.

  1. NOTE: Hoses with an auxiliary transmission fluid cooler shown, hoses without an auxiliary transmission fluid cooler similar.

    Disconnect the 2 transmission fluid cooler hoses from the radiator.

3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. If equipped, detach the auxiliary transmission fluid cooler from the A/C condenser and position aside.

6.2L engine

  1. Using a suction device, syphon the power steering fluid from the power steering fluid reservoir.
  1. Release the clamp and disconnect the power steering fluid cooler hose from the power steering fluid cooler.

All engines

  1. Remove the radiator/condenser core as an assembly.
  1. Release the lock tabs and separate the radiator from the condenser core.

Installation

All engines

  1. Position the condenser core into the radiator locking tabs.
  1. Position the radiator/condenser core assembly into the vehicle and onto the radiator insulators.

6.2L engine

  1. Connect the power steering cooler hose to the power steering cooler and position the clamp.

All engines

  1. Install the 2 radiator bolts.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).

3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. If equipped, attach the auxiliary transmission fluid cooler to the A/C condenser.

All engines

  1. Connect the transmission fluid cooler hoses to the radiator.
  1. Connect the wiring harness retainer.
  1. Connect the transmission fluid cooler tubes.
  1. Install the transmission cooler tube secondary latches.
  1. Using new gasket seals and O-ring seals, connect the A/C condenser fittings and install the bolt and nut.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).

3.5L GTDI engines

  1. Position the lower air deflector, attach the 8 pushpins and turn the 4 quarter-turn retainers.

3.7L, 5.0L and 6.2L engines

  1. Install the 4 lower radiator air deflector-to-condenser pushpins.

All vehicles

  1. Connect the 3 RH and 2 LH air deflector-to-condenser pushpin retainers.

3.5L Gasoline Turbocharged Direct Injection (GTDI) , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Install the degas bottle and the 2 bolts.
    • Tighten to 17 Nm (150 lb-in).
  1. Connect the MAF sensor electrical connector.
  1. Push the spring clip in place and connect the lower radiator hose quick connect coupling to the radiator, making sure the coupling is secure.
  1. Connect the radiator-to-degas bottle hose to the radiator and position the clamp.
  1. Connect the upper radiator hose to the radiator and position the clamp.

6.2L engines

  1. Install lower radiator hose and the spring clip.
  1. Connect the radiator overflow hose.
  1. Connect the upper radiator hose and position the clamp.

All engines

  1. Install the cooling fan motor and shroud. For additional information, refer to Cooling Fan Motor and Shroud in this section.

6.2L engine

  1. Fill the power steering fluid reservoir with MERCON® V.

All engines

  1. Fill the cooling system. For additional information, refer to Cooling System Draining, Filling and Bleeding in this section.
  1. Recharge the A/C system. For additional information, refer to Section  412-00.
  1. Check the transmission fluid and fill as necessary. For additional information, refer to Section  307-01.
