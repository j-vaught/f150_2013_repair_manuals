Removal

3.5L GTDI engine (if equipped with block mounted heater), 5.0L and 6.2L engines

  1. Drain the engine cooling system. For additional information, refer to Cooling System Draining, Filling and Bleeding in this section.

  1. NOTE: Inspect and replace this splash shield if it is damaged or will not re-secure properly to the vehicle.

    If equipped, release the 4 fasteners and remove the splash shield.

3.5L Gasoline Turbocharged Direct Injection (GTDI) and 3.7L engines (if equipped with thermostat housing mounted heater)

  1. Remove the air cleaner outlet pipe. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.

  1. NOTICE: To install, route the block heater power cable away from hot or rotating components, or the cable can be damaged.

    Disconnect the block heater electrical connector.

3.5L Gasoline Turbocharged Direct Injection (GTDI) and 3.7L engines (if equipped with thermostat housing mounted heater)

  1. NOTICE: To install, route the block heater power cable away from hot or rotating components, or the cable can be damaged.

    Disconnect the block heater electrical connector.
  1. Remove the bolt and the block heater.
    • To install, tighten to 6 Nm (53 lb-in).

3.5L GTDI engine (if equipped with block mounted heater)

  1. NOTICE: To install, route the block heater power cable away from hot or rotating components, or the cable can be damaged.

    Disconnect the block heater electrical connector.
  1. Using the block heater socket remove the block heater.

5.0L engine (4X2)

  1. NOTICE: To install, route the block heater power cable away from hot or rotating components, or the cable can be damaged.

    Disconnect the block heater electrical connector.
  1. Remove the block heater.
    • To install, tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).

5.0L engine (4X4)

  1. Remove the generator. For additional information, refer to Section  414-00 in this section.

  1. NOTICE: To install, route the block heater power cable away from hot or rotating components, or the cable can be damaged.

    Disconnect the block heater electrical connector.
  1. Remove the block heater.
    • To install, tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).

6.2L engine

  1. NOTICE: To install, route the block heater power cable away from hot or rotating components, or the cable can be damaged.

    Disconnect the block heater electrical connector.
  1. Remove the block heater.
    • To install, tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).

Installation

3.5L Gasoline Turbocharged Direct Injection (GTDI) and 3.7L engines (if equipped with thermostat housing mounted heater)

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

3.5L GTDI engine (if equipped with block mounted heater)

  1. Using the block heater socket install the block heater.
    • Tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).
  1. Connect the block heater electrical connector.

  1. NOTE: Inspect and replace this splash shield if it is damaged or will not re-secure properly to the vehicle.

    If equipped, install the splash shield and the 4 fasteners.

5.0L and 6.2L engines

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
