Removal and Installation

All engines

  1. Disconnect the battery negative cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Remove the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet tube. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Remove the 4 pushpins and the upper radiator air deflector.

6.2L engines

  1. Disconnect the Mass Air Flow (MAF) sensor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 2 bolts and position the coolant expansion tank aside.
  1. Detach the power steering fluid cooler hose retainer from the cooling fan motor and shroud.
  1. Detach the wiring harness retainer from the power steering fluid reservoir stud bolt.
  1. Remove the nut, detach the generator battery positive cable from the positive battery terminal, detach the 3 wiring harness retainers from the cooling fan shroud and position the wiring harness aside.
    • To install, tighten to 9 Nm (80 lb-in).
  1. Detach the upper radiator hose retainer from the cooling fan motor and shroud.

3.5L Gasoline Turbocharged Direct Injection (GTDI) , 3.7L and 5.0L engines

  1. Detach the wiring harness retainer from the stud bolt on the LH side of the cooling fan shroud.
  1. Disconnect the positive battery cable. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Detach the battery cable retainer from the cooling fan shroud.

All engines

  1. NOTE: Push the 2 LH wiring harness retainer tabs outward to release.

    Detach the LH wiring harness retainer and the 3 RH wiring harness pushpins.
  1. Disconnect the 2 cooling fan electrical connectors.
  1. Detach the 3 cooling fan wiring harness retainers.
  1. Remove the 4 Battery Junction Box (BJB) bracket bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).

3.5L GTDI , 3.7L and 5.0L Engines

  1. Position the wiring harness aside.

6.2L engines

  1. Remove the power steering fluid reservoir stud bolt and position the power steering fluid reservoir and the wiring harness aside.
    • To install, tighten to 11 Nm (97 lb-in).

All engines

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and the cooling fan motor and shroud.
    • To install, tighten to 7 Nm (62 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
