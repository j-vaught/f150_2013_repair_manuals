303-03 Engine Cooling 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 07/28/2015

Coolant Crossover Manifold Assembly — 3.5L GTDI
Item Part Number Description
1 W527362W527362 Heater hose clamp
2 1847218472 Heater hose
3 W714988W714988 Intake manifold bolt
4 W503279W503279 Coolant crossover manifold assembly bolt (3 required)
5 8C3688C368 Coolant crossover manifold assembly
6 94399439 Coolant crossover manifold assembly gasket (2 required)

Removal

  1. Drain the cooling system. For additional information, refer to Cooling System Draining, Filling and Bleeding in this section.
  1. Remove the intake manifold. For additional Information, refer to Section  303-01A.
  1. Release the clamp and disconnect the heater hose from the coolant crossover manifold assembly.
  1. Remove the 3 bolts and the coolant crossover manifold assembly.
    • Remove and discard the gaskets.

Installation

  1. Install new gaskets, position the coolant crossover manifold assembly and install the bolts finger tight.
  1. Tighten the bolts in 2 stages.
    • Stage 1: Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
    • Stage 2: Tighten an additional 45 degrees.
  1. Connect the heater hose and position the clamp.
  1. Install the intake manifold. For additional Information, refer to Section  303-01A.
  1. Fill and bleed the cooling system. For additional information, refer to Cooling System Draining, Filling and Bleeding in this section.
