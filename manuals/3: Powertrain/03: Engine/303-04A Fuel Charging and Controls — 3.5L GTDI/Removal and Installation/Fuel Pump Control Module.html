303-04A Fuel Charging and Controls — 3.5L GTDI 2013 F-150

Fuel Pump Control Module


Item Part Number Description
1 — Fuel Pump Control Module (FPCM) electrical connector (part of 14404)
2 W706027W706027 FPCM bolt (2 required)
3 93459345 FPCM

Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect theFuel Pump Control Module (FPCM) electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 2 bolts and the FPCM .
    • To install, tighten to 9 Nm (80 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
