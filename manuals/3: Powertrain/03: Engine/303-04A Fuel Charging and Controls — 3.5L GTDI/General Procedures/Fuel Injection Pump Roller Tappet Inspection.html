303-04A Fuel Charging and Controls — 3.5L GTDI 2013 F-150
General Procedures Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Fuel Injection Pump Roller Tappet Inspection

  1. Inspect the fuel injection pump tappet for flat spots or scoring. If any damage is found, inspect the fuel injection pump and the fuel injection pump tappet drive lobe. Install new components as necessary.
