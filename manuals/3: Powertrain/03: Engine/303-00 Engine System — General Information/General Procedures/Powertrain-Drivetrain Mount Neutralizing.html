Powertrain/Drivetrain Mount Neutralizing

NOTE: Refer to the appropriate section and procedure for special instructions on loosening and tightening mount fasteners.

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information, refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Loosen, but do not remove, the powertrain/drivetrain mount fasteners.
  1. Lower the vehicle.

  1. NOTICE: Do not twist or strain the powertrain/drivetrain mounts or damage to the mounts may occur.

    Start the vehicle and move it in forward 0.6-1.2 m (2-4 ft). Then move the vehicle in reverse the same distance.
  1. Raise and support the vehicle.
  1. Tighten the powertrain/drivetrain mount fasteners.
  1. Lower the vehicle.
  1. Test the system for normal operation.
