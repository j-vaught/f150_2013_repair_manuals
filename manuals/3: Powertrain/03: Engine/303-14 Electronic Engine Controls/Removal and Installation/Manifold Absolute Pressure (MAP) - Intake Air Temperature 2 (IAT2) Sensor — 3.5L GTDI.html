Removal

NOTE: The Turbocharger Boost Pressure (TCBP) /Charge Air Cooler Temperature (CACT) sensor and the Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP) / Intake Air Temperature 2 (IAT2) sensor are not interchangeable.

  1. Disconnect the MAP /IAT2 sensor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the bolt and the MAP /IAT2 sensor.

Installation

  1. Lubricate the MAP /IAT2 sensor O-ring seal with clean engine oil.
  1. Install the MAP /IAT2 sensor and the bolt.
    • Tighten to 6 Nm (53 lb-in).
  1. Connect the MAP /IAT2 sensor electrical connector.
