303-14 Electronic Engine Controls 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 09/23/2014

Crankshaft Position (CKP) Sensor — 5.0L (4V)

Removal

  1. Remove the intake manifold. For additional information, refer to Section  303-01C.
  1. Disconnect the Cylinder Head Temperature (CHT) sensor electrical connector.
  1. Disconnect theCrankshaft Position (CKP) sensor electrical connector.

  1. NOTE: The Crankshaft Position (CKP) sensor bolt is part of the CKP sensor and cannot be removed.

    Loosen the bolt and remove the CKP sensor.

Installation

  1. NOTE: The Crankshaft Position (CKP) sensor bolt is part of the CKP sensor and cannot be removed.

    Position the CKP sensor and tighten the bolt.
    • Tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
  1. Connect the CKP sensor electrical connector.
  1. Connect the CHT sensor electrical connector.
  1. Install the intake manifold. For additional information, refer to Section  303-01C.
  1. After completing the repairs, use a diagnostic scan tool to perform the Misfire Monitor Neutral Profile Correction procedure.
