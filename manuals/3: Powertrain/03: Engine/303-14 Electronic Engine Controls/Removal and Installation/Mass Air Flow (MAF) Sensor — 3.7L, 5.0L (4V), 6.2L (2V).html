303-14 Electronic Engine Controls 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Mass Air Flow (MAF) Sensor — 3.7L, 5.0L (4V), 6.2L (2V)
Item Part Number Description
1 — Mass Air Flow (MAF) sensor electrical connector (part of 12A581)
2 W709287W709287 MAF sensor screw (2 required)
3 12B57912B579 MAF sensor

Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect the Mass Air Flow (MAF) sensor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 2 screws and the MAF sensor.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
