Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the engine Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet pipe. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Remove the bolt for the intake manifold vacuum tube.
    • Detach the intake manifold vacuum tube pin-type retainer from the Throttle Body (TB) .
    • To install, tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
  1. Disconnect the electronic throttle control electrical connector.
    • Detach the electronic throttle control electrical connector wiring harness pin-type retainer.
  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the TB .
    • Discard the TB gasket.
    • To install, tighten to 10 Nm (89 lb-in).

  1. NOTE: Install a new TB gasket.

    To install, reverse the removal procedure.
