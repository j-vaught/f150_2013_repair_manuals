303-07A Engine Ignition — 3.5L GTDI 2013 F-150
Description and Operation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Engine Ignition

The Electronic Ignition (EI) system is a coil-on-plug ignition system. The coil-on-plug ignition system consists of the:

  • six ignition coil-on-plugs.
  • six spark plugs.

The 6 separate ignition coil-on-plugs:

  • change low voltage signals from the PCM to high voltage pulses.
  • produce the high voltage pulses to the spark plugs.
  • are connected directly to the spark plug.
  • have replaceable ignition coil-on-plug seals.

The 6 spark plugs:

  • change high voltage pulses into a spark which ignites the fuel and air mixture.
  • originally equipped on the vehicle have a iridium-enhanced fine wire electrode for long life.
  • gap is not adjustable.
