206-07 Power Brake Actuation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Brake Booster Vacuum Sensor

NOTE: Rear Wheel Drive (RWD) shown, Four-Wheel Drive (4WD) similar.


Item Part Number Description
1 — Vacuum sensor electrical connector (part of 12A581)
2 23652365 Brake booster vacuum sensor
3 — Hose clamp (part of 9C490)
4 — Vacuum supply hose (part of 9C490)

  1. Relieve the vacuum in the brake booster by pressing and releasing the brake pedal a minimum of 5 times with the engine not running.
  1. Disconnect the vacuum supply hose from the brake booster vacuum sensor.
  1. If equipped, disconnect the Four-Wheel Drive (4WD) vacuum reservoir supply hose from the brake booster vacuum sensor.
  1. Disconnect the vacuum sensor electrical connector and remove the vacuum sensor.

  1. NOTE: To allow proper drainage of any moisture, the vacuum sensor must be installed in the same position and orientation as removed.

    To install, reverse the removal procedure.
