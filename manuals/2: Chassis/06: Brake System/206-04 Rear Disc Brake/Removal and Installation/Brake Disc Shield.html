206-04 Rear Disc Brake 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Brake Disc Shield

Removal

NOTE: Removal steps in this procedure may contain installation details.

  1. Remove the rear axle. For additional information, refer to Section  205-02A or Section  205-02B.
  1. Remove the parking brake shoes. For additional information, refer to Section  206-05.
  1. To install, tighten to 14 Nm (124 lb-in).

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
