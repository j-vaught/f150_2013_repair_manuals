206-05 Parking Brake and Actuation 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Parking Brake Cable — Rear, RH

Removal

NOTE: Removal steps in this procedure may contain installation details.

  1. Release the parking brake cable tension. For additional information, refer to Parking Brake Cable Tension Release in this section.
  1. To install, tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. To install, tighten to 30 Nm (22 lb-ft).

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Inspect the parking brake system for correct operation.
