206-05 Parking Brake and Actuation 2013 F-150
General Procedures Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Parking Brake Shoe Adjustment


Special Tool(s)
Brake Adjusting Gauge
206-D002 (D81L-1103-A) or equivalent

  1. NOTE: Make sure the parking brake is fully released.

    Using the release handle, release the parking brake control.
  1. Remove the rear brake disc. For additional information, refer to Section  206-04.
  1. Using the Brake Adjusting Gauge, measure the ID of the drum portion of the rear brake disc and set the locking screw.
    • Record the measurement.
  1. Place the Brake Adjusting Gauge over the widest diameter of the parking brake shoes.
  1. Adjust the parking brake shoe clearance to 0.59 mm (0.023 in) less than the ID of the drum portion of the rear brake disc.
    • Rotate the parking brake shoe adjuster to achieve the correct parking brake shoe-to-brake disc clearance.
  1. Install the rear brake disc. For additional information, refer to Section  206-04.
  1. Test the parking brake for normal operation.
