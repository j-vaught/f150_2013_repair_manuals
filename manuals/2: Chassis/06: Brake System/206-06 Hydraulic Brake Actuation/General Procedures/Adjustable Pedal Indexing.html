206-06 Hydraulic Brake Actuation 2013 F-150
General Procedures Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Adjustable Pedal Indexing

NOTICE: The adjustable pedal system must be indexed whenever the brake pedal assembly or accelerator pedal assembly is installed or damage to the components may result.

  1. NOTE: Make sure the electrical connector is connected to the adjustable pedal motor.

    Disconnect the accelerator pedal-to-brake pedal cable from the adjustable brake pedal gear set.
  1. Operate the accelerator pedal to the full rearward position.
  1. Connect the adjustable pedal motor drive cable to the adjustable brake pedal drive.
  1. Operate the adjustable pedals to the full forward position.
  1. Disconnect the adjustable pedal motor drive cable from the adjustable brake pedal assembly.
  1. Operate the adjustable accelerator pedal to the full forward position.
  1. Connect the adjustable pedal motor drive cable to the adjustable brake pedal drive.
  1. Check that the brake and accelerator pedals can be fully adjusted forward and rearward.
