206-03 Front Disc Brake 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Brake Disc Shield

Removal

  1. Remove the brake disc. For additional information, refer to Brake Disc in this section.
    • To install, tighten to 18 Nm (159 lb-in).

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
