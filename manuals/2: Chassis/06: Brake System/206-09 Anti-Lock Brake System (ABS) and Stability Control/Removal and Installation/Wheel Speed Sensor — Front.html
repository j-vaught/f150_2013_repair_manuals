206-09 Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS) and Stability Control 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Wheel Speed Sensor — Front
Item Part Number Description
1 W710279W710279 Wheel speed sensor harness bolt
2 — Wheel speed sensor bolt (part of 2C204)
3 2C2042C204 Wheel speed sensor
4 — Wheel speed sensor electrical connector (part of 14250)

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the brake disc. For additional information, refer to Section  206-03.

  1. NOTE: The wheel speed sensor harness electrical connector is located in the engine compartment secured to the fender apron.

    Disconnect the wheel speed sensor electrical connector.
  1. Detach the 2 wheel speed sensor harness pushpin retainers.
  1. Detach the wheel speed sensor harness retainer from the brake hose.
  1. Remove the wheel speed sensor harness bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
  1. Remove the wheel speed sensor bolt and the wheel speed sensor.
    • To install, tighten to 17 Nm (150 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
