Disassembly

  1. Remove the differential carrier. Tag the differential shims and bearing cups for location. Refer to Differential Carrier.
  1. Remove and discard the 12 differential ring gear bolts.

  1. NOTICE: Care should be taken not to damage the differential ring gear bolt hole threads.

    Insert a punch in the differential ring gear bolt holes. Drive off the differential ring gear.
  1. If checking differential case flange runout, install the bearing cups in their original location and install the differential assembly.
    1. Install the bearing caps and differential shims.
      • Rotate the differential assembly to verify the bearings have seated.
    1. Install the Dial Indicator Gauge With Holding Fixture and record the differential case runout.

  1. NOTE: The differential bearings need not be removed to overhaul the Ford limited slip differential.

    If necessary, use the 2 Jaw Puller and Step Plate to remove the differential bearings.
  1. If traction-Lok clutch packs need to be removed, refer to Traction-Lok Clutch Packs

Assembly

All vehicles

  1. If traction-Lok clutch packs were removed, refer to Traction-Lok Clutch Packs
  1. Install the 2 new differential bearings using the Differential Carrier Bearing Installer.

Electronic Locking Differential (ELD) equipped vehicles and all vehicles equipped with 3.55, 3.73, and 4.10 ratio gear sets

  1. NOTE: The differential flange and ring gear flange must be free of any old retaining compound material. Make sure both surfaces are clean and free of oil, dust and debris. Failure to clean the surfaces can result in ring gear runout concerns.

    Clean all traces of the old retaining compound material from the differential flange.
    • Use solvent and Scotch-Brite® pads to remove.
  1. Apply a one-eighth inch bead of maximum strength retaining compound on the rear face of the ring gear in the pattern shown.

All vehicles

  1. Using a suitable press, install the differential ring gear.
  1. Install the new 12 differential ring gear bolts.
    • Tighten to 150 Nm (111 lb-ft).
  1. Install the differential carrier. Refer to Differential Carrier.
