Removal

  1. Remove the differential carrier. For additional information, refer to Differential Carrier in this section.
  1. Using the Bearing Puller plate, Step Plate and a suitable press, remove the LH differential bearing.
  1. Remove and discard the 6 screws and 3 stoppers that hold the Electronic Locking Differential (ELD) coil in place and remove the coil.
  1. Remove and discard the plunger assembly.

Installation

  1. Install a new plunger assembly.
  1. Install the new ELD coil, 3 stoppers and 6 screws that hold the coil in place.
    • Tighten to 5 Nm (44 lb-in).
  1. Use the Differential Carrier Bearing Installer to install the LH differential bearing.
  1. Install the differential carrier. For additional information, refer to Differential Carrier in this section.
