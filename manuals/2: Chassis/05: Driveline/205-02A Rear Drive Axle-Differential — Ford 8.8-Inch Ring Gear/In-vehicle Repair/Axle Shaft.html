Removal and Installation

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information, refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Remove the rear wheel and tire. For additional information, refer to Section  204-04.
  1. Remove the rear brake disc. For additional information, refer to Section  206-04.
    • To install, tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. Remove the differential housing cover and drain the lubricant. For additional information, refer to Differential Housing Cover in this section.
  1. Discard the specified component. Follow local disposal regulations.
    • To install, tighten to 30 Nm (22 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Inspect and lubricate the lip of the wheel bearing oil seal with grease.
