211-02 Power Steering 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Steering Gear to Fluid Cooler Return Hose — To Cooler

Removal

NOTICE: While repairing the power steering system, care should be taken to prevent the entry of foreign material or failure of the power steering components may result.

NOTE: Removal steps in this procedure may include installation details.

  1. On both sides.
    • To install, tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. Discard the specified component. Follow local disposal regulations.

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. Fill the power steering system. For additional information, refer to Section  211-00B.
