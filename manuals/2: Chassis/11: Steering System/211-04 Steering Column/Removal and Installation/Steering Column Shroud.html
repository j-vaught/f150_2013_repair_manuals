211-04 Steering Column 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Steering Column Shroud


Item Part Number Description
1 W710485W710485 Lower steering column shroud screw (3 required)
2 3530B3530B Lower steering column shroud
3 3530A3530A Upper steering column shroud

Removal

  1. Remove the 3 screws and the steering column opening trim panel.
  1. Place transmission selector lever in PARK.
  1. Position the steering column in the full downward and rearward position.
  1. Apply the parking brake.
  1. With the ignition key in the ON position and the brake pedal pressed, shift the transmission selector lever into DRIVE.

  1. NOTICE: Use care when detaching the transmission selector lever boot clips from steering column shrouds or damage to the selector lever boot may occur causing replacement of the shift lever assembly. The transmission selector lever boot is not serviceable separately.

    Position the selector lever boot aside.
    1. Push forward on the selector lever boot at the area indicated and separate the boot from the upper and lower steering column shrouds.
  1. Pull upward on the rearward portion of the upper steering column shroud to separate the clips from the lower steering column shroud.
    • Roll the upper steering column shroud upward on the hinge point and then pull straight rearward to remove the upper shroud.
  1. Shift the shift lever to PARK and remove the ignition key.
  1. Remove the 3 screws and the lower steering column shroud.

Installation

  1. Position the lower steering column shroud and install the 3 screws.
    • Tighten to 2 Nm (18 lb-in).
  1. Position the upper steering column shroud onto the lower steering column shroud and secure the front clips.

  1. NOTICE: Use care and follow the installation sequence when installing the selector lever boot to the steering column shrouds or damage to the boot may occur causing replacement of the selector lever assembly. The selector lever boot is not serviceable separately.

    Install the selector lever boot in the sequence shown.
    1. Slide the front of the selector lever boot into position.
    1. Slide the bottom of the selector lever boot into position.
    1. Slide the top of the selector lever boot into position and push down.
    1. Push down on the back of the selector lever boot until selector lever boot is fully seated.
  1. Place transmission selector lever in PARK.
  1. Release the parking brake.
  1. Position the steering column opening trim panel and install the 3 screws.
