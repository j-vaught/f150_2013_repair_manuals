211-04 Steering Column 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Steering Wheel


Item Part Number Description
1 W709344W709344 Steering wheel bolt
2 — Steering wheel switches electrical connector (part of 3600)
3 36003600 Steering wheel

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the driver air bag module. For additional information, refer to Section  501-20B.
  1. Turn the steering wheel to the straight-ahead position.
  1. Remove and discard the steering wheel bolt.
    • To install, tighten the new bolt to 48 Nm (35 lb-ft).
  1. Disconnect the steering wheel switches electrical connector from the clockspring.
  1. Remove the steering wheel while routing the wires from the clockspring through the steering wheel.
  1. Tape the clockspring center rotor to the outer housing to prevent it from rotating.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
