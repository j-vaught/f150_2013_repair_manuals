211-05 Steering Column Switches 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Ignition Switch

NOTE: Manual column shown, power column similar.


Item Part Number Description
1 — Ignition switch electrical connector (part of 14401)
2 1157211572 Ignition switch

Removal and Installation

Manual and Power Columns

  1. Remove the steering column shrouds. For additional information, refer to Section  211-04.
  1. Disconnect the ignition switch electrical connector.

Manual Columns

  1. Place the steering column tilt lever in the unlocked position.

Manual and Power Columns

  1. Press the 2 locking tabs and remove the ignition switch.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
