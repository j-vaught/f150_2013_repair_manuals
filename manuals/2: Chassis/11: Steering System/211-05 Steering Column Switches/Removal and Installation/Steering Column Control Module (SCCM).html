211-05 Steering Column Switches 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Steering Column Control Module (SCCM)


Item Part Number Description
1 — Steering Column Control Module (SCCM) electrical connectors (3 required) (part of 14401)
2 W715057W715057 SCCM bolts (3 required)
3 3F7913F791 SCCM

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the steering wheel rotation sensor. For additional information, refer to Section  206-09.
  1. Disconnect the 3 Steering Column Control Module (SCCM) electrical connectors.
  1. Remove the 3 screws and the SCCM .
    • To install, tighten to 2 Nm (18 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
