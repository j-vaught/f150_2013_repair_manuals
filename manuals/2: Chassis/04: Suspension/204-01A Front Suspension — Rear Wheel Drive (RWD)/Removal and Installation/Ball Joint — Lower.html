Item Part Number Description
1 30503050 Lower ball joint
2 — Lower ball joint snap ring (part of 3050 kit)

Removal

  1. Remove the wheel knuckle. For additional information, refer to Wheel Knuckle in this section.
  1. Remove and discard the lower ball joint snap ring.
  1. Using the C-Frame and Screw Installer/Remover and Ball Joint Remover, remove the lower ball joint.

Installation

  1. NOTICE: Do not damage the lower ball joint boot when installing the C-Frame and Screw Installer/Remover and Ball Joint Installer/Remover or premature failure of the ball joint may occur.

    NOTE: Make sure the ball joint snap ring is fully seated.

    Using the C-Frame and Screw Installer/Remover and Ball Joint Installer/Remover, install the lower ball joint.
    • Install the new ball joint snap ring.
  1. Install the wheel knuckle. For additional information, refer to Wheel Knuckle in this section.
