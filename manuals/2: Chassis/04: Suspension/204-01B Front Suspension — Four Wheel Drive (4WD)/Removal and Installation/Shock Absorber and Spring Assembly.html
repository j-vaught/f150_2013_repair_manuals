Removal

NOTICE: Suspension fasteners are critical parts because they affect performance of vital components and systems and their failure may result in major service expense. New parts must be installed with the same part numbers or equivalent parts, if replacement is necessary. Do not use a replacement part of lesser quality or substitute design. Torque values must be used as specified during reassembly to make sure of correct retention of these parts.

All vehicles

  1. Remove and discard the shock absorber upper mount nuts.
  1. Remove the wheel and tire. Refer to Section  204-04.

SVT Raptor

  1. Remove the shock absorber and spring assembly upper spacer.
  1. Remove the wheel bearing and wheel hub. Refer to Wheel Bearing and Wheel Hub in this section.

All vehicles

  1. Remove and discard the tie-rod end nut.
  1. Using the Ball Joint Separator, separate the tie-rod end from the wheel knuckle.
  1. Remove and discard the upper ball joint nut.
  1. Remove and discard the shock absorber lower nut and bolt.
  1. Using the Ball Joint Separator, disconnect the upper arm ball joint from the wheel knuckle and remove the shock absorber and spring assembly.

SVT Raptor

  1. Remove the lock washer and the shock absorber and spring assembly lower spacer.
    • Discard the lock washer.

All vehicles

  1. NOTE: If the individual spring and/or shock components are not being serviced, continue to the installation procedure.

    NOTE: For reference during assembly, index the upper mount, spring and shock absorber.

    Using a suitable spring compressor, compress the spring until the tension is released from the shock absorber.

  1. NOTE: Use the hex-holding feature to prevent the shock rod from turning while removing the nut.

    While holding the shock rod, remove the nut and the shock absorber.
    • Discard the nut.
  1. Remove the upper mount, dust boot and insulator.

Installation

All vehicles

NOTICE: Do not tighten the lower shock nut until the installation procedure is complete and the weight of the vehicle is resting on the wheel and tire assemblies or incorrect clamp load and bushing damage may occur.

  1. Position the shock absorber and spring and install the dust boot (if equipped), insulator and the upper mount.

  1. NOTE: Align the index marks made during disassembly.

    Using a suitable spring compressor, compress the spring until the tension is released from the shock absorber.

  1. NOTE: Use the hex-holding feature to prevent the shock rod from turning while installing the nut.

    While holding the shock rod, install the new shock absorber and the shock rod nut.
    • Tighten to 55 Nm (41 lb-ft).

SVT Raptor

  1. NOTE: The thickest part of the lower spacer is towards to inside of the vehicle.

    Install the shock absorber and spring assembly lower spacer and a new lock washer.

All vehicles

  1. NOTE: Do not tighten the nut at this time.

    Position the shock absorber and loosely install the new shock absorber lower nut and bolt.
  1. Connect the upper arm ball joint to the wheel knuckle and install the new nut.
    • Tighten to 115 Nm (85 lb-ft).
  1. Install the new tie-rod end nut.
    • Tighten to 115 Nm (85 lb-ft).

SVT Raptor

  1. Install the wheel bearing and wheel hub. Refer to Wheel Bearing and Wheel Hub in this section.

  1. NOTE: The thickest part of the upper spacer is towards to outside of the vehicle.

    Install the shock absorber and spring assembly upper spacer.

All vehicles

  1. Install the wheel and tire. Refer to Section  204-04.
  1. Install the new shock absorber upper mount nuts.
    • Tighten to 40 Nm (30 lb-ft).
  1. With the weight of the vehicle on the wheel and tire, tighten the shock absorber lower nut to 550 Nm (406 lb-ft).
