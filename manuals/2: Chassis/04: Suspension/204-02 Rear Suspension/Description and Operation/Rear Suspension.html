204-02 Rear Suspension 2013 F-150
Description and Operation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Rear Suspension

The rear suspension system consists of the following components:

  • Axle jounce bumpers
  • Semi-elliptic, leaf-type springs
  • Shock absorbers
  • Wheel studs

The rear suspension system utilizes a solid driving axle supported by shock absorbers and semi-elliptic, leaf-type rear springs.
