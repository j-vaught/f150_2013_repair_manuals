204-04 Wheels and Tires 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Tire Pressure Monitor (TPM) Module


Item Part Number Description
1 19A43519A435 TPMS module mounting bracket
2 W707628W707628 TPMS screw (1 required)
3 15K60215K602 Tire Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS) module
4 — TPMS module electrical connector (part of 14401)
5 — External antenna coax connector

Removal

NOTICE: Electronic modules are sensitive to static electrical charges. If exposed to these charges, damage may result.

  1. Remove the RH instrument panel side finish panel.
  1. Open the glove compartment door and while pushing in on the glove compartment tab, position the glove compartment downwards.
  1. Remove the screw from the TPMS module mounting bracket.
  1. Disconnect the TPMS module electrical connector and the external antenna connector.
  1. Separate the TPMS module from the TPMS module mounting bracket.

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
