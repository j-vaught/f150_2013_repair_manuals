413-06 Horn 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Horn


Item Part Number Description
1 — Horn electrical connector (part of 12A581)
2 W709435W709435 Horn bolt
3 1383213832 Horn

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the bolt and the horn assembly.
    • Disconnect the electrical connector.
    • To install, tighten to 35 Nm (26 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
