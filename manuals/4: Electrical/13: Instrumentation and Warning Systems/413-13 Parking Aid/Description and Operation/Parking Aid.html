413-13 Parking Aid 2013 F-150
Description and Operation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Parking Aid

The parking aid system consists of the following:

  • Parking aid sensors (located in the rear bumper)
  • Parking Aid Module (PAM) (located on the cowl above and next to the brake pedal)
  • Parking aid speaker (integral to the PAM )

  • NOTE: Parking aid disable feature is not available when a MyKey® is in use.

    Parking aid disable feature (integral to the Instrument Panel Cluster (IPC) message center)

The parking aid system uses of 4 ultrasonic sensors to calculate the distance to an object around the rear of the vehicle. The parking aid system sounds a warning to alert the driver of stationary objects near the rear bumper when REVERSE (R) is selected.

Rear Video

The rear video camera system consists of the following:

  • Rear video camera (located in the blue oval Ford emblem housing in the tailgate)
  • Auto-dimming interior mirror (without navigation)
  • Front Display Interface Module (FDIM) (with navigation)
  • IPC
  • Body Control Module (BCM)
  • Camera switching module (if equipped with the front video camera)

The rear video camera system visually aids the driver while reversing or reverse parking the vehicle.

For vehicles without navigation, the image from the rear video camera is displayed in the left portion of the auto-dimming interior mirror. For vehicles with navigation, the image from the rear video camera is displayed in the FDIM .

The rear video camera system features are as follows:

  • Fixed guidelines — assists the driver with backing into a parking space or aligning the vehicle with an object.
  • Visual park aid alert — assists the driver to visually see the object causing the parking aid system to sound.
  • Manual zoom — assists the driver with connecting or towing a trailer and allowing the driver to manually zoom closer to an object behind the vehicle.
  • Video camera delay — allows the driver to see the image behind the vehicle after the vehicle is shifted out of REVERSE (R). The image is not displayed if the vehicle is shifted into PARK (P).

Rear Video Camera System With Base IPC

For vehicles equipped with a base IPC (2 rows, 14 character display), only fixed guidelines are displayed in the auto-dimming interior mirror and cannot be disabled.

Rear Video Camera System With Optional IPC

For vehicles equipped with an optional IPC (4.2 LCD message center) include all the rear video camera system features and the features are enabled and disabled within the message center.

Rear Video Camera System With Navigation

For vehicles equipped with navigation include all the rear video camera system features. These features are enabled and disabled within the FDIM .

Refer to the Owner's Literature for information on rear video camera system operation.

Front Video — F-150 Raptor Only

The front video camera system consists of the following:

  • Front video camera (located in the front grille).
  • FDIM
  • IPC
  • BCM
  • Camera switching module (under dash left of the steering column)

The front video camera system visually aids the driver during off-road driving at vehicle speeds less than 24 km/h (15 mph).

The image from the front video camera is displayed in the FDIM .

The front video camera system features are as follows:

  • Fixed guidelines — assists the driver to align with an object in front of the vehicle.
  • Active guidelines — assists the driver with show the path of intended forward motion of the vehicle while in D (Drive).
  • Manual zoom — assists drivers as an off road aid. It allows the driver to manually zoom closer to an object in front of the vehicle. The zoomed image keeps the bumper in the image to provide a reference.

Refer to the Owner's Literature for information on the video camera system operation.
