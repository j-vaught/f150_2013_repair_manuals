Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the instrument panel side finish panel.

  1. NOTE: The instrument panel dimmer switch is removed by pushing from behind.

    Remove the instrument panel dimmer switch and bezel assembly from the instrument panel.
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
  1. Release the tabs and separate the instrument panel dimmer switch from the bezel.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
