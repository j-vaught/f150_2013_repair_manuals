Instrument Panel Cluster (IPC) Lens


Item Part Number Description
1 N805589N805589 Instrument Cluster (IC) screws (2 required)
2 1089010890 IC lens
3 1084910849 IC

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the Instrument Cluster (IC) . For additional information, refer to Instrument Panel Cluster (IPC) in this section.
  1. Remove the 2 screws.
  1. Release the 7 tabs and remove the IC lens.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
