413-01 Instrumentation, Message Center, and Warning Chimes 2013 F-150
General Procedures Procedure revision date: 06/01/2015

Message Center Configuration

Oil Life Reset — Message Center Instrument Cluster (IC)

  1. Press and release the SETUP button until the message center displays OIL LIFE XXX% HOLD RESET = NEW OIL.
  1. Press and hold the RESET button for 2 seconds and release when the message center displays OIL LIFE SET TO XXX%.
  1. Press and release the SETUP button to exit the procedure.

Oil Life Reset — Base Instrument Cluster (IC)

NOTE: All message center functions are carried out using the single stem SELECT/RESET button in the Instrument Cluster (IC) .

  1. Press and release the SELECT/RESET button to scroll through the information displays until the message center displays HOLD RESET FOR SETUP MENU.
  1. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET button for approximately 2 seconds until the message center displays OIL LIFE XXX% HOLD RESET = NEW.
  1. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET button for approximately 2 seconds until the message center displays OIL LIFE SET TO XXX%.
  1. Release the SELECT/RESET button and allow the setup timer to expire to exit the procedure.

Oil Life Start Value — Message Center Instrument Cluster (IC)

  1. Press and release the SETUP button until the message center displays OIL LIFE XXX% HOLD RESET = NEW OIL.
  1. Press and hold the RESET button for 2 seconds and release when the message center displays OIL LIFE SET TO XXX%.
  1. Press and release the RESET button to decrease the oil life start value (from 100% to 10%) for each 10% reduction until the desired oil life start value is displayed.
  1. Press and release the SETUP button to exit the procedure.

Oil Life Start Value — Base Instrument Cluster (IC)

NOTE: All message center functions are carried out using the single stem SELECT/RESET button in the IC .

  1. Press and release the SELECT/RESET button to scroll through the information displays until the message center displays HOLD RESET FOR SETUP MENU.
  1. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET button for approximately 2 seconds until the message center displays OIL LIFE XXX% HOLD RESET = NEW.
  1. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET button for approximately 2 seconds until the message center displays OIL LIFE SET TO XXX%.

  1. NOTE: The oil life start value reduces by 10% for each second that the SELECT/RESET button is held once the oil life start value begins decreasing.

    Continue to hold the SELECT/RESET button until the oil life begins to decrease in value (from 100% down to 10%), continuing to hold until the desired start value is displayed.
  1. Release the SELECT/RESET button and allow the setup timer to expire to exit the procedure.
