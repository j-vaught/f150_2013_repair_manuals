414-01 Battery, Mounting and Cables 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Battery Tray

  1. Remove the battery. For additional information, refer to Battery and Battery Tray — Exploded View in this section.
  1. Disconnect the wire harness retainers from the battery tray and position the wire harness aside.
  1. Remove the 3 bolts from the battery tray and carefully position the battery tray aside to gain access to the washer reservoir electrical connector.
    • To install, tighten to 17 Nm (150 lb-in).
  1. Disconnect the wire connector and washer hose from the washer bottle and remove the battery tray assembly.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
