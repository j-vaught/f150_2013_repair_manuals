414-05 High Voltage Converter/Power Point Inverter 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Direct Current/Alternating Current (DC/AC) Inverter


Item Part Number Description
1 W707628W707628 DC/AC inverter screw (3 required)
2 0430404304 Instrument panel
3 19G31719G317 DC/AC inverter
4 — DC/AC inverter electrical connector (part of 14401)

WARNING: Disconnect the 12 volt battery before servicing the direct current to alternating current (DC-AC) inverter or alternating current (AC) powerpoint to prevent the risk of high voltage shock. Failure to follow this instruction may result in serious personal injury.

  1. Remove the center instrument panel lower finish panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the LH center instrument panel support bracket.
    • To install, tighten to 9 Nm (80 lb-in).
  1. Disconnect the DC/AC inverter electrical connectors.
  1. Remove the 3 screws and the DC/AC inverter.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
