414-05 High Voltage Converter/Power Point Inverter 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Alternating Current (AC) Power Point


Item Part Number Description

2 0602406024 Storage compartment door

6 19N23619N236 AC powerpoint

8 W705289W705289 Floor console rear panel retaining clip (2 required)

WARNING: Disconnect the 12 volt battery before servicing the direct current to alternating current (DC-AC) inverter or alternating current (AC) powerpoint to prevent the risk of high voltage shock. Failure to follow this instruction may result in serious personal injury.

  1. Remove the 3 screws and the storage compartment door.

  1. NOTICE: Raise the selector lever trim panel only enough to access the floor console rear panel screws. If the selector lever trim panel is raised excessively, component damage may occur.

    Lift the rear portion of the selector lever trim panel to release the retaining clips.
  1. Remove the 2 floor console rear panel screws.
  1. Using a non-marring tool, gently pry the lower portion of the floor console rear panel to release the 2 retaining clips.
  1. Disconnect the AC powerpoint electrical connector.
  1. Press the AC powerpoint retainers inward and remove the AC powerpoint.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
