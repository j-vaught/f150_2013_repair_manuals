414-05 High Voltage Converter/Power Point Inverter 2013 F-150
Description and Operation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

High-Voltage Converter/Inverter

DC/AC Inverter and AC Power Point

The high-voltage converter/inverter system consists of the following components:

  • AC power point
  • DC/AC inverter

The DC/AC inverter converts low voltage (12 volts) DC to 110 volts AC to power a device that uses AC power with a rating of less than 150 watts. The DC/AC inverter is located behind the instrument panel, and is a serviceable part. The 110-volt AC power point is located in the rear of the front floor console and can be serviced separately from the front floor console.
