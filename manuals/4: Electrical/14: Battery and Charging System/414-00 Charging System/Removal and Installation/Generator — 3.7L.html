Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect the battery. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Remove the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet pipe. For additional information, refer to Section  303-12.
  1. Rotate the Front End Accessory Drive (FEAD) belt tensioner counterclockwise and position the FEAD belt aside.
  1. Disconnect the generator electrical connector.
  1. Position the generator B+ protective boot aside, remove the B+ nut and position the B+ terminal aside.
    • To install, tighten to 17 Nm (150 lb-in).
  1. Remove the generator stud nut.
    • To install, tighten to 48 Nm (35 lb-ft).
  1. Remove the generator bolt and the generator.
    • To install, tighten to 48 Nm (35 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
