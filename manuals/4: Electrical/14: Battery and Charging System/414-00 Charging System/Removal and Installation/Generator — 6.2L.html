414-00 Charging System 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Generator — 6.2L
Item Part Number Description
1 W715092W715092 Generator bolt (3 required)
2 1015310153 Generator bracket
3 W711953W711953 Generator B+ terminal nut
4 — Generator B+ terminal (part of 14305)
5 — Generator electrical connector (part of 14305)
6 1030010300 Generator
7 86208620 Accessory drive belt
8 6B2096B209 Accessory drive belt tensioner

  1. Disconnect the battery. For additional information, refer to Section  414-01.
  1. Loosen the Air Cleaner (ACL) outlet pipe clamp at the ACL upper tray and remove the ACL outlet pipe and resonator assembly. For additional information, refer to Intake Air System Components - Exploded View in Section  303-12.
  1. Rotate the accessory drive belt tensioner clockwise and position the accessory drive belt aside.
  1. Disconnect the generator electrical connector.
  1. Position the generator B+ protective boot aside, remove the generator B+ terminal nut and position the generator B+ terminal aside.
    • To install, tighten to 17 Nm (150 lb-in).
  1. Remove the 3 bolts, generator bracket and the generator.
    • To install, tighten to 47 Nm (35 lb-ft).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
