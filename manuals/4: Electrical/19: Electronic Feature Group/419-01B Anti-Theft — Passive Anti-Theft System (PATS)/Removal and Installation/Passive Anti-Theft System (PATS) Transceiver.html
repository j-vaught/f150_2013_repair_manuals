419-01B Anti-Theft — Passive Anti-Theft System (PATS) 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Passive Anti-Theft System (PATS) Transceiver


Item Part Number Description
1 W710485W710485-S-S Steering column shroud screws (3 required)
2 35333533 Steering column shroud, lower
3 — Passive Anti-Theft System (PATS) transceiver electrical connector (part of 14401)
4 1560715607 PATS transceiver

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the 3 screws and the lower steering column shroud.
  1. Disconnect the Passive Anti-Theft System (PATS) transceiver electrical connector.
  1. Using a suitable tool (such as a dentist pick), pry the PATS transceiver tangs outward and remove the PATS transceiver.

  1. NOTE: Replacement of the PATS transceiver does not require the PATS keys to be programmed into the Instrument Cluster (IC) again.

    To install, reverse the removal procedure.
