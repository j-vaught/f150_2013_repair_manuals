415-00F Information and Entertainment Systems — Ford Work Solutions 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Telematics Module

Removal

  1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
  1. Remove the Instrument Panel Cluster (IPC) . Refer to Section  413-01.
  1. Remove the 2 bolts and position the panel down.
  1. Remove the 2 screws and the telematics module.
    • Disconnect the electrical connector.

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. Initialize the telematics module.
    1. Wait 5 minutes after the modem-antenna is connected.
    1. Turn the ignition to RUN.
    1. Wait 10 seconds for the airbag light to turn off.
    1. Start the vehicle and run the engine for 30 seconds.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    1. Repeat steps 4 and 5 two more times for a total of 3 ignition key cycles.
  1. Go to the technician service publications web site. On the On-Line Automotive Service Information System (OASIS) tab, select the "FW Status" tab.
    1. Enter data in at least one of the required fields.
    1. All 4 green status indicators will illuminate to validate the telematics module installation. If all 4 of the green status indicators do not illuminate, refer to Information and Entertainment System.
