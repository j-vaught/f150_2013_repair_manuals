415-00F Information and Entertainment Systems — Ford Work Solutions 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Modem — Antenna

Removal

NOTE: For vehicles with an in-dash computer, the customer must contact the internet service provider to re-activate the account after the modem-antenna has been replaced. The account must be re-activated before internet services can operate correctly.

  1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
  1. Remove the LH instrument panel side finish panel.
  1. Remove the modem-antenna bolt and the modem-antennna.
    • Disconnect the electrical connector.
    • To install, tighten to 30 Nm (22 lb-ft).

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. Initialize the modem-antenna.
    1. Connect all electrical connectors.
    1. Wait 5 minutes after the modem-antenna is installed.
    1. Turn the ignition to RUN.
    1. Wait 10 seconds.
    1. Start the vehicle and run the engine for 30 seconds.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
