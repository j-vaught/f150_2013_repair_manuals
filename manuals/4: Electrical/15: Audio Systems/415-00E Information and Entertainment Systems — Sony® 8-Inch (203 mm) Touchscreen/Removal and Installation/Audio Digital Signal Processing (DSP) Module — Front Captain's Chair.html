Audio Digital Signal Processing (DSP) Module — Front Captain's Chair
Item Part Number Description
1 — Audio Digital Signal Processing (DSP) module bolts (3 required)
2 18B84918B849 Audio DSP module
3 — Audio DSP module electrical connectors (part of 14A005)

Removal and Installation

  1. NOTE: Module configuration is required when a new audio Digital Signal Processing (DSP) module is being installed.

    Upload the audio DSP module configuration information to the scan tool. For additional information, refer to Programmable Module Installation (PMI) in Section  418-01.
  1. Remove the floor console. For additional information, refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Disconnect the audio DSP module electrical connectors.
  1. Remove the 3 bolts and the audio DSP module.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Download the configuration information to the audio DSP module. For additional information, refer to PMI in Section  418-01.
