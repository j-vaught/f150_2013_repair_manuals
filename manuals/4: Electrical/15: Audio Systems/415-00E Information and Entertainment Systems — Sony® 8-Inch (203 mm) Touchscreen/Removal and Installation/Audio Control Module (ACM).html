Audio Control Module (ACM)

Removal and Installation

  1. NOTE: If installing a new module, it is necessary to upload the module configuration information to the scan tool prior to removing the module. This information must be downloaded into the new module after installation.

    Upload the Audio Front Control Module (ACM) configuration information to the scan tool by following the scan tool on screen instructions.
  1. Remove the Front Display Interface Module (FDIM) . Refer to Front Display Interface Module (FDIM).
  1. Remove the 4 ACM bolts and the ACM .
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
    • To install, tighten to 2.5 Nm (23 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. Download the ACM configuration information from the scan tool to the new ACM following the scan tool on screen instructions.
