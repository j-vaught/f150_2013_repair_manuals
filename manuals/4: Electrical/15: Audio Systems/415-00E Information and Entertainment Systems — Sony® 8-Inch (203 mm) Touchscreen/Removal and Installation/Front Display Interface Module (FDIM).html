415-00E Information and Entertainment Systems — Sony® 8-Inch (203 mm) Touchscreen 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 06/25/2013

Front Display Interface Module (FDIM)

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the Front Controls Interface Module (FCIM) . Front Controls Interface Module (FCIM).
  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the Front Display Interface Module (FDIM) .
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
    • To install, tighten to 2.5 Nm (23 lb-in).
  1. Remove the 4 screws and remove the Accessory Protocol Interface Module (APIM) from the FDIM .
    • To install, tighten to 0.6 Nm (5 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. Calibrate the FDIM touchscreen. Refer to Touchscreen Calibration.
