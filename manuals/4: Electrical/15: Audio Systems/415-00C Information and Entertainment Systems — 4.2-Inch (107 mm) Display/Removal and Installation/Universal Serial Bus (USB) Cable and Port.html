415-00C Information and Entertainment Systems — 4.2-Inch (107 mm) Display 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Universal Serial Bus (USB) Cable and Port

Removal and Installation

Instrument Panel USB Cable Without Floor Console

  1. Remove the APIM . Refer to Accessory Protocol Interface Module (APIM).
  1. Remove the audio input jack USB bezel.
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
  1. For access remove the glove box. Refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Remove the USB cable following the routing shown.

Instrument Panel USB Cable With Floor Console

  1. Remove the APIM . Refer to Accessory Protocol Interface Module (APIM).
  1. For access remove the glove box. Refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Remove the RH lower floor console trim panel.
  1. Remove the USB cable following the routing shown.

Floor Console USB Cable

  1. Open the floor console stowage bin door.
  1. Remove the audio input jack USB bezel from the floor console.
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
  1. Remove the RH lower floor console trim panel.
  1. Remove the floor console upper finish panel.
  1. Remove the floor console USB cable following the routing shown.

All Cables

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
