Accessory Protocol Interface Module (APIM)


Item Part Number Description
1 W704875W704875-S-S Accessory Protocol Interface Module (APIM) screw
2 — APIM electrical connector (part of 14401)
3 — Universal Serial Bus (USB) cable (part of 14D202)
4 14D21214D212 APIM

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the instrument panel upper bin.
    1. Remove the rubber mat.
    1. Remove the 2 screws.
    1. Remove the instrument panel upper bin.
  1. Remove the APIM screw and position the APIM upward.
  1. Remove the APIM .
    • Disconnect the electrical connector and the Universal Serial Bus (USB) cable.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • If a new APIM is being installed, program the APIM . For additional information, refer to Accessory Protocol Interface Module (APIM) Programming.
