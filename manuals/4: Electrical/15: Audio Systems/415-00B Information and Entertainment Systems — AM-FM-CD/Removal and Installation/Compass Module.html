Compass Module

Removal and Installation

NOTE: The compass module is glued to the headliner.

NOTE: When replacing the compass module, obtain 3M™ Duramix™ 4747 Super Fast Adhesive commercially to attach the compass module to the headliner. The 3M™ Duramix™ 4747 Super Fast Adhesives is a 2- part adhesive in a 50 ml dual-tube applicator.

  1. Remove the headliner. Refer to Section  501-05.
  1. Remove the compass module from the headliner.
    • Disconnect the electical connector.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  1. CALIBRATE the compass. Refer to General Procedures in Section  413-01.
