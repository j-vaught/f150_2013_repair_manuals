Removal and Installation

  1. NOTE: Module configuration is required when a new Front Display Interface Module (FDIM) is being installed.

    Upload the FDIM configuration information to the scan tool . Refer to Programmable Module Installation (PMI) in Section  418-01.
  1. Remove the mat, the 2 screws, and the instrument panel upper bin.
  1. Remove the 4 screws and the FDIM .
    • Disconnect the electrical connector(s).
    • To install, tighten to 2 Nm (18 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Download the configuration information to the FDIM . Refer to PMI in Section  418-01.
