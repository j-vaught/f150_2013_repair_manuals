Removal and Installation

NOTE: It is not necessary to remove the Audio Front Control Module (ACM) to retrieve the part number. Refer to Audio Control Module (ACM) Self-Diagnostic Mode in this section.

  1. NOTE: Module configuration is required when a new ACM is being installed.

    Upload the ACM configuration information to the scan tool . Refer to Programmable Module Installation (PMI) in Section  418-01.
  1. Remove the Front Controls Interface Module (FCIM) . Refer to Front Controls Interface Module (FCIM).
  1. Remove the 4 screws and the ACM .
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
    • To install, tighten to 2.5 Nm (23 lb-in).
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Download the configuration information to the ACM . Refer to PMI in Section  418-01.
