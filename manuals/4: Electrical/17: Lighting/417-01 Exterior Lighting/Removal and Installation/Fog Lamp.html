417-01 Exterior Lighting 2013 F-150

Fog Lamp


Item Part Number Description
1 — Fog lamp electrical connector (part of 12A581)
2 W505424W505424 Fog lamp bolts (3 required)
3 1520015200/15201/15201 Fog lamp assembly (RH/LH)

Removal and Installation

NOTE: Make sure the fog lamp switch and ignition switch are in the OFF position.

  1. With the vehicle in NEUTRAL, position it on a hoist. For additional information, refer to Section  100-02.
  1. Disconnect the fog lamp electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 3 bolts and the fog lamp bracket.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
