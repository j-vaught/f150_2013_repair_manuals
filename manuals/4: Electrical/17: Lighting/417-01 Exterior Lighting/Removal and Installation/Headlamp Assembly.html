417-01 Exterior Lighting 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Headlamp Assembly


Item Part Number Description
1 W703277W703277 Headlamp bolts (3 required)
2 1300813008 Headlamp assembly
3 — Electrical connectors (part of 12A581)

Removal and Installation

NOTE: Make sure the headlamp switch and the ignition switch are in the OFF position.

  1. Remove the pin-type retainer and position the radiator side air deflector aside.

  1. NOTE: Grasp the headlamp assembly firmly and pull forward to release the 2 metal clips.

    Remove the 3 bolts and the headlamp assembly.
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
