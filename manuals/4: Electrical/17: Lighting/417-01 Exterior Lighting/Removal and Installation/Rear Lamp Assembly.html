Rear Lamp Assembly

NOTE: Styleside lamp shown, flareside similar.

4 1341113411 Rear stop/turn lamp bulb holder

6 1341113411 Reversing lamp bulb holder
7 1346613466 Reversing lamp bulb

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the 2 rear lamp assembly bolts.
    • To install, tighten to 5 Nm (44 lb-in).
  1. Firmly pull the rear lamp assembly rearward to disengage the metal studs from the grommets on the outboard side of the sheet metal.
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
  1. If necessary, rotate the bulb holder counterclockwise approximately one-eighth turn and remove it.
    • Separate the bulb from the bulb holder.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
