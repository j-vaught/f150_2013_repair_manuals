417-02 Interior Lighting 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Interior Lamp

Item Part Number Description

2 54519A7054519A70 Overhead console
3 — Overhead console electrical connectors (part of 14358)



Front Lamp (Vehicles Without Overhead Console) and Rear Lamp


Item Part Number Description
1 1378313783 Interior lamp lens

3 — Interior lamp electrical connector (part of 14358)

Removal

Front lamp (vehicles with overhead console)

  1. NOTE: Do not pull-down on the front of the overhead console.

    Pull-down on the rear of the overhead console to release the overhead console from the retaining clips.
    • Disconnect the electrical connectors.
  1. Release the 4 retaining clips, and remove the lamp assembly from the overhead console.
  1. Release the 6 retaining clips, and remove the lens from the lamp assembly.

    • NOTE: The bulbs are removed by pushing from the backside of the lamp assembly.

      Replace the bulb(s) as necessary.

Front lamp (vehicles without overhead console) or rear lamp

  1. NOTE: The tabs (located just above the headliner) can be accessed with a small angled pick tool. They are released by pulling them away from the lamp assembly. Use care not to use too much pressure or the tabs may break.

    Release the 3 locking tabs and remove the interior lamp lens.
  1. Release the 2 retaining clips (through the access holes) and remove the interior lamp.
    • Disconnect the electrical connector.

Installation

Front lamp (vehicles with overhead console)

  1. Install the lens onto the lamp assembly.
  1. Install the lamp assembly into the overhead console.
  1. Connect the electrical connectors and install the overhead console.

Front lamp (vehicles without overhead console) or rear lamp

  1. Install the interior lamp lens.
  1. Connect the electrical connector and install the interior lamp.
