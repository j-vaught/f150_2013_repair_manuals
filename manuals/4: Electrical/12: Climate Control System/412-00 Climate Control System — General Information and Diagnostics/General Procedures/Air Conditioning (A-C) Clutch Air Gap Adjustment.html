General Procedures Procedure revision date: 02/09/2016

Air Conditioning (A/C) Clutch Air Gap Adjustment

  1. Check the A/C clutch air gap at 3 equally spaced places between the clutch plate and the A/C clutch pulley.
    • Air gap clearance 0.3-0.6 mm (0.012-0.024 in)
  1. Using the universal clutch holding tool, hold the A/C clutch disc and hub, remove the A/C clutch disc and bolt. Add or remove spacers between the clutch plate hub and the compressor shaft until the clearance is within specification.
    • Tighten to 18 Nm (159 lb-in)
