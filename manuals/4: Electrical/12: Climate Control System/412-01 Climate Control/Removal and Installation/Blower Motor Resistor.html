Blower Motor Resistor


Item Part Number Description
1 — Blower motor resistor electrical connector (part of 18B518)
2 W701696W701696 Blower motor resistor screw (2 required)
3 1859118591 Blower motor resistor

Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect the blower motor resistor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 2 blower motor resistor screws.
  1. Remove the blower motor resistor.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
