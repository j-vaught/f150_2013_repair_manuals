412-01 Climate Control 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Mode Door Actuator — Air Inlet Door
Item Part Number Description
1 023A98023A98 Junction box cover
2 W701696W701696 Air inlet mode door actuator screw (3 required)
3 — Air inlet mode door actuator electrical connector (part of 18B574)
4 19E61619E616 Air inlet mode door actuator

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the junction box cover.
  1. Disconnect the air inlet mode door actuator electrical connector.
  1. Remove the 3 air inlet mode door actuator screws.
  1. Remove the air inlet door mode door actuator.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
