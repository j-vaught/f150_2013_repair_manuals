In-Vehicle Temperature and Humidity Sensor


Item Part Number Description

7 19C73419C734 In-vehicle temperature and humidity sensor

Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the 3 steering column cover screws and the steering column cover.
  1. Remove the 4 steering column cover reinforcement bolts.
  1. Detach the in-vehicle temperature and humidity sensor aspirator hose.
  1. Disconnect the in-vehicle temperature and humidity sensor electrical connector.
  1. Remove the in-vehicle temperature and humidity sensor.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
