412-01 Climate Control 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Heating Ventilation Air Conditioning (HVAC) Module

Removal and Installation

NOTE: If installing a new HVAC module, the new module must be configured for the vehicle. For additional information, refer to Section  418-01.

With Electronic Automatic Temperature Control or Electronic Manual Temperature Control Remote Mount

  1. Remove the upper instrument panel storage tray mat.
  1. Remove the 2 upper instrument panel storage tray screws.
  1. Disconnect the speaker electrical connector (if equipped), and remove the upper instrument panel storage tray.
  1. Remove the HVAC module.
    • Disconnect the HVAC module electrical connectors.
    • Remove the 4 HVAC module screws.

Vehicles with Electronic Manual Temperature Control Base

  1. Remove the instrument panel finish panel – center, lower. For additional information, refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Remove the HVAC module.
    • Remove the 4 HVAC module screws.

All vehicles

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
