Removal and Installation

NOTE: If an evaporator core leak is suspected, the evaporator core must be vacuum leak tested before it is removed from the vehicle.

NOTE: If a heater core leak is suspected, the heater core must be leak tested before it is removed from the vehicle.

NOTE: Failure to correctly install the evaporator core seal when installing the evaporator core will result in decreased A/C cooling efficiency due to airflow bypassing the evaporator core.

  1. Drain the engine coolant. For additional information, refer to Section  303-03.
  1. Recover the refrigerant. For additional information, refer to Section  412-00.
  1. Remove the instrument panel. For additional information, refer to Section  501-12.
  1. Disconnect the 2 heater core quick disconnect fittings at the heater core.
  1. Remove the Thermostatic Expansion Valve (TXV) manifold and tube bracket bolt.
    • To install, tighten to 8 Nm (71 lb-in).
  1. Remove the TXV fitting nut and disconnect the fitting.
    • Discard the gasket seals.
    • To install, tighten to 15 Nm (133 lb-in).
  1. Remove the 3 heater core and evaporator core housing nuts.
    • To install, tighten to 7 Nm (62 lb-in).
  1. Detach the 3 harness electrical connectors from the heater core and evaporator core housing bracket.
  1. If equipped, detach the 2 antenna cable retainers from the heater core and evaporator core housing.
  1. Remove the air inlet duct bracket nut.
    • To install, tighten to 7 Nm (62 lb-in).
  1. Completely loosen the plenum chamber nut.
    • To install, tighten to 7 Nm (62 lb-in).
  1. If equipped, detach the floor console duct from the heater core and evaporator core housing.

  1. NOTE: For vehicles equipped with a rear footwell duct, the heater core and evaporator core housing must be carefully detached from the dash panel and then tilted toward the rear of the vehicle to detach it from the rear footwell duct connection.

    Remove the heater core and evaporator core housing.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Install new O-ring seals.
    • Add the correct amount of clean PAG oil to the refrigerant system. For additional information, refer to the Refrigerant Oil Adding procedure in Section  412-00.
  1. Fill the engine cooling system. For additional information, refer to Section  303-03.
