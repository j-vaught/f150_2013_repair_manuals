412-01 Climate Control 2013 F-150
Removal and Installation Procedure revision date: 08/30/2012

Register


Item Part Number Description
1 1989319893 Register assembly

Removal

  1. Using a suitable 90 degree pick-type tool, individually release the 4 clips while gently pulling the register outward to keep the clips from reengaging.
  1. Remove the register.

Installation

  1. Install the register by aligning it with the opening in the instrument panel and carefully pushing it into the opening until the retaining clips engage.
