Removal

  1. Remove the A/C compressor. For additional information, refer to Air Conditioning (A/C) Compressor in this section.
  1. Remove the A/C clutch disc and hub bolt.
    1. Using the A/C Clutch Holding Tool, hold the A/C clutch disc and hub.
    1. Remove the A/C clutch disc and hub bolt.
  1. Remove the A/C clutch disc and hub.
  1. Remove the A/C compressor pulley snap ring.

  1. NOTICE: Do not use air tools. Damage to the Air Conditioning (A/C) clutch pulley or A/C compressor may result.

    Using the Compressor Pulley Remover, remove the A/C compressor pulley.
    1. Install the Compressor Pulley Remover.
    1. Remove the A/C compressor pulley.
  1. Remove the A/C clutch field coil snap ring.
  1. Remove the A/C clutch field coil electrical connector screw.
  1. Remove the A/C clutch field coil.

Installation

NOTE: A new A/C compressor may come equipped with a clutch and pulley. If not, the A/C clutch components should be reused unless obvious signs of damage are found. If excessive grooving is found on the friction surface, a new A/C clutch disc and hub and a new A/C compressor pulley must be installed together. Otherwise, each component can be installed individually where needed.

  1. Visually inspect the A/C clutch disc and hub, A/C compressor pulley and A/C clutch field coil for damage.
    • Inspect for physical damage, including cracked or melted components or discoloration due to excessive heat.
    • Inspect for excessive wear, including grooving in the A/C clutch disc and hub or A/C compressor pulley that is more than fingernail depth.
    • Inspect for roughness in the A/C compressor pulley bearing.
  1. Clean the A/C clutch field coil and pulley mounting surfaces.
  1. Install the A/C clutch field coil.
  1. Install the A/C clutch field coil electrical connector screw.
  1. Install the A/C clutch field coil snap ring.

  1. NOTE: The A/C compressor pulley is a tight fit on the A/C compressor. It must be correctly aligned during installation.

    Install the A/C compressor pulley.
  1. Install the A/C compressor pulley snap ring with the bevel side out.
  1. Place one nominal thickness A/C clutch disc and hub spacer inside the clutch hub spline opening.
  1. Install the A/C clutch disc and hub.
  1. Using the A/C Clutch Holding Tool, install the A/C compressor clutch disc and hub bolt.
    1. Hold the A/C clutch disc and hub with the A/C Clutch Holding Tool.
    1. Tighten the bolt to 14 Nm (124 lb-in).
  1. Measure and adjust the clutch air gap at 3 spaces by removing or adding A/C clutch disc and hub spacers.
  1. Install the A/C compressor. For additional information, refer to Air Conditioning (A/C) Compressor in this section.
